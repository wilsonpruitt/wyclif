## For the coordinator

1. **K02a-1: wrong locus.** Loserth's plate reads *ST* III q. 76 (not 77) and *In IV* d. 10 q. 1 a. 2. Neither treats quantity as the subject. The right places are *ST* III q. 77 a. 2 and *In IV* d. 12 q. 1 a. 1 qc. 3.
2. **K01e-6: untraced.** The Benedicite is our conjecture. Wyclif uses *cathalogus sanctorum* elsewhere for the roll of the saints. Decide whether to keep the Benedicite in n17 at all.
3. **K02a-4: Wyclif's number differs from the canon.** He has 114 (twice). Friedberg's canon has CXIII (113), with CXX in Ivo.
4. **Still open:** K01e-2 and K02b-2, the ordinary gloss, which is not local. K02a-2, the Scotus scholium number. K01e-7, Vallarsi II, 824, not checked; Loserth's Hedibia reference may belong to another sentence.

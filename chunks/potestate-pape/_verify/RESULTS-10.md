## For the coordinator

1. **P10k-1: the homily number.** The passage closes Migne's hom. 35 (PG 56, 832), not hom. 28. Wyclif's XXVIII may follow an older numbering. Loserth's "Opp. VI, 771" was not checked.
2. **P10k-4: the letter is to Gregory IX.** Ep. 35 is not addressed to Innocent IV, so the note's identification needs the correction above.
3. **P10l-1: Luard's number is 72, not 37.** "Letter 37" is Wyclif's numbering, and he calls it "73" in *De civ. dom.* III. The note conflated it with Luard's.
4. **P10k-6: decided as Grosseteste's treatise.** Wyclif has no work of the title. The phrase *in materia de cessacione legalium* is still partly topical, so the coordinator may soften "is" to "is the title of".
5. **P10l-3 and P10l-5 are only partly checked.** The Digest/*Liber Extra* loci for *par in parem* and the Sinuessa *Gesta* are not local.
6. **P11a-2: Wyclif's "end of book 3" monster** remains unlocated; book 3's only *monstrum* is III.4.17.

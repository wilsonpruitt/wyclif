## Doubtful, left as they are

- **12-s ¶1, p. 386**: *Alii autem dicunt quod est quantitas, alii vero quod est qualitas*, rendered "And some say". (c), illustrating the "bare teaching" the caesarean prelates would put forth, fits; (b) "But some say" is possible only if read as against Wyclif's own view, which is not stated here. "And" kept.
- **12-s ¶2, p. 386**: *Possunt autem abusiones Romani pontificis reduci ad numerum duodecim*, rendered "And". Either (c), continuing the case against the caesarean pope, or (a), "Now", the next step that opens the list of twelve abuses. A toss-up; "And" defensible.
- **12-t ¶1, p. 389** (changed above): *Inficitur autem* could be read as (c), extending the account of the office; changed to "Now" because *Ideo caveret papa … ab istis duobus criminibus* draws the conclusion from it.
- **12-u ¶2, p. 395**: *Sed mirantur nonnulli, quare Romana ecclesia non canonizat Constantinum*, rendered "But". (b), against the preceding account of Gregory's venial sin for endowment (yet the Roman church honours the endower less than the East), or a new-topic "now". "But" kept.
- **12-u ¶4, p. 396**: *Sed (si non fallor) facere illud officium foret clero minus inconsonum*, rendered "But". A correcting *sed* (upward, close to *ymmo*), but the card's adversative "but" carries it.

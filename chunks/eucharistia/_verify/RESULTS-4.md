## For the coordinator

1. **The Bernard sentence is found (K02c-3 and K02c-4).** Wyclif's *Manus ille que fundaverunt celos* is in a sermon printed as Peter Damian's, *Sermo* 47 (PL 144, 763D). Its title and incipit are exactly as Wyclif gives them. prol-a n13 (Kprola-5) still says *not found in PL 182–185*. It should now give PL 144, 761C–766B and 763D. Several of the *Damian* sermons in PL 144 are commonly assigned to Nicholas of Clairvaux, Bernard's secretary, which would explain why the sermon circulated under Bernard's name. I did not check this attribution: decide whether to say it.
2. **Loserth is wrong at K02c-5 and K02d-5.** The Patripassians are in *De haeresibus* 41. *Precavens in futurum* is a medieval Vulgate reading, not a non-Vulgate one.
3. **K02c-1 is not certain.** *De potestate pape* is the only printed exposition of Apoc. 19:8 that I found, but Wyclif's *alias diffuse exposui* may mean his unprinted Apocalypse postill.
4. **Still open.** K02e-6: the definition of transubstantiation is untraced, and the ordinary gloss on X 1.1.1 is needed. K02e-1: the Maurist columns V, 144 and I, 630 were not checked.

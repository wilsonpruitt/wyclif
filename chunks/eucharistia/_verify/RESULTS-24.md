## For the coordinator

1. **K09a-5 / K09a-6.** Loserth's Scotus locus (d. 8 q. 2) is wrong for 274.6. The passage is d. 11 q. 3, and it names two decretals, which explains Wyclif's plural. The LEDGER should carry d. 11 q. 3.
2. **K09a-12.** *Estote subditi* is Gratian's dictum, not Ambrose. Wyclif's attribution follows manuscripts that lack the dictum sign. The note now says so. Check that you accept the reworded n21.
3. **K09a-11.** The print's *Medium* (DE *Meum*) at 275.7 may be a corruption of Gratian's inscription *Idem* (*Idem in commentariis ad c. 5 Lucae*), not a word of the canon. This is a conjecture only. The emendation in n20 stands either way.
4. **K09a-13.** D. 20 c. 1 (Leo IV, *Aliorum scripturae Romanorum Pontificum decretis non sunt preponendae*) says the opposite of Wyclif's point. His *capitulum primum* must mean the opening dictum, as at 280.2 (*in principio*).
5. **K09b-2.** Marked verified on a representative instance, since *sepe* names a habit and not a single place. Downgrade to untraced if you want a single locus.
6. **K09b-3.** *Gloriosus Deus* (VI 3.22 un.) is the only canon that names *four* doctors, but Wyclif says *decretum*. I left it out of the note.
7. **K09c-6.** Loserth's p. 164 is a weak parallel. Decide whether the note should point to pp. 161–162 and 194–195 as well.

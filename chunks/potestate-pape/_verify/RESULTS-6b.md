## For the coordinator to decide

1. **P08a-2: a wrong attribution.** The note gives Ep. 112 to "Augustine's collection". It is Jerome's Ep. 112 (PL 22, 916), which is Augustine's Ep. 75. The fix is above.
2. **P08a-1.** "Cf. Huss, 256" is unchecked, because the Hus edition Loserth meant is not identified. Loserth also cites Freeman, *William Rufus* I, 292, and the note omits this. Should it be added?
3. **P08a-8 / P08a-5.** The empire list in VII.16 is spoken by FitzRalph's interlocutor John, while Wyclif says *declarat dominus Ardmacanus*. The proposed note says so. VII.16 (fol. 48r–v) also has Jerusalem *in medio terre* with Ps. 73:12 and the martyrs of Jerusalem. That is Wyclif's own counter at 167.1–8 and 172.14 ff., taken from FitzRalph's objector. Should 08-a n8 and the 08-b ¶ at 172 note this?
4. **P08a-11.** As in P06h-1, Higden's voice is the devil's (*hostis antiquus*). The 08-a n18 phrasing "a voice was heard" is neutral, so it can stand.
5. **P08b-4.** Loserth reports *libro III* in the manuscripts at 170.21, where the print has *libro II, III*. Bernard is book II. Should a T note record this?
6. **P08a-4 and P08b-7** stay untraced. The *Rosarium* text was not available to search.

## Changes

Quamvis **autem** hoc sit argumentum ad hominem … grave tamen … foret hereticare in hoc omnes de secta secunda* | And although this is an | Now although this is an | (a) the ad hominem argument has closed with *ergo*. ## Doubtful, left as they are

- **05-i, p. 147**: *maximum inconveniens … foret quod idem corpus moveretur simul et semel ad diversa loca, **sed** possibilitatem huius oportet viam istam concedere, nam …*, rendered "But this way must grant the possibility of this." Read as (b): it turns the opponent's absurdity back on his own position. It could be (a), the premiss of an implied reductio ("so it is no absurdity against us"), but no *ergo* follows. Left.
- **05-j, p. 153**: *Locucio **autem** tropica est verissima*, rendered "But tropical speech is most true". Read as (b): it contradicts the objector's premiss that tropical speech is false. It could also be (a), since it is the step that denies the quibble *in materia*, with *Nec est color …* then denying it *in forma*. This is a real toss-up between "But" and "Now". Left.
- **05-j, p. 153**: ***Sed** stulti sic arguentes obliti racionis argumenti …, ideo oportet acute respondere*, rendered "But the fools who argue so". The *ideo* draws only on this sentence's own clause and does not close a syllogism, so (b) stands: the reply set against the rejoinder. Left.

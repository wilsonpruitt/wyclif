## Doubtful, left as they are

- **pen06 ¶1, p. 341**: *Literalis autem secundum principia Augustini habet summam auctoritatem*, rendered "Now". Either (a), the premiss brought to bear on the lepers argument and closed by *Concedenda est ergo historia*, or (c), the next item in the account of the fourfold sense ("And the literal"). The following *ergo* makes "Now" defensible.
- **quest ¶1, p. 347**: *Alii autem infideles taciti dicunt*, rendered "And other unbelievers". Either (c), the next item after the vagabond objections, or (b), the silent unbelievers set against the stated faith ("But other unbelievers"). "And" fits the run (*Nec sunt obiectus … Alii autem*), and the true contrast is carried by the *Sed tacent* that follows.

## SOURCE ITEMS (Friedberg II)
A. Extrav. Jo. XXII tit. IX *De torneamentis* c. un. *Quia in futurorum eventibus*: CONFIRMED (Lyons, XVI Kal. Oct., year 1 = 1316). Wording: "sane felicis recordationis Clemens Papa V. praedecessor noster ... torneamenta et hastiludia sive iuxtas in regnis Franciae, Angliae et Alemanniae ... specialiter interdixit ... in personas excommunicationis, et in terras interdicti sententias promulgando"; John then "sententias excommunicationis et interdicti praefatas ... revocamus", absolving those who incurred them. So E23k-3: the earlier pope is Clement V (prohibition tied to the Vienne passagium). Wyclif's "alius papa prius" = Clement V.
B. X 5.38.4 = *Quod autem consuluisti* (Idem to the Archbishop of Canterbury): "remissiones ... prodesse illis tantummodo arbitramur, quibus ... proprii iudices specialiter indulserunt" (indulgences of one not their judge do not avail), the canon limiting a bishop's grants to his own subjects. *Nostro postulasti* is X 5.38.15 (Honorius III: an archbishop may grant letters through his province, within the Council's limit), NOT .4. X 5.38.14 = *Quum ex eo* (Lateran IV c.62, 40 days dedication/anniversary limit), confirmed.
C. Clem. 5.9.2 *Abusionibus* (Clement V, Vienne): CONFIRMED; quaestores admitted only on apostolic or diocesan letters, may preach nothing beyond "quod in literis continebitur"; §1 lists abuses (indulgences, vow dispensations, souls from purgatory, "a poena et a culpa"); §2 bishops to punish. Fits 584.27-30 exactly. (Friedberg heads it Clem. V tit. IX c. II.)
D. Aug. Ep. 82 to Jerome (PL 33, ed. 7300.xml): at §21 (after pb 0285) "officiosum mendacium ... sitne aliquando mentiri viri boni" and §22 "ut ... inconcusse credatur ... abesse mendacium" in canonical scripture. The sentence closest to "if any falsity is found in scripture, authority perishes" is, however, in Ep. 28 §3 (PL 33 col. 113): "Admisso enim semel in tantum auctoritatis fastigium officioso aliquo mendacio, nulla illorum librorum particula remanebit..." (also quoted back in Jerome's reply). The codices' "epistola nona" may point to Ep. 28 (Maurist numbering not checked); Loserth's "LXXXII (alias XIX)" matches Ep. 82. Recommend the note cite both: Ep. 82 §§21-22 and Ep. 28 §3.

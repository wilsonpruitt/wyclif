## For the coordinator to decide

1. **I07b-7.** The source settles nothing between print and A B C: Gregory's chapter is 19 (older 11), neither 15 nor 3. The source does support *dici* (om. A B C).
2. **07-b n14.** Two of Wyclif's three *Moralia* loci are wrong. "30.5" is XXXII.5 and "34.5" is XXXIV.7. The replacement text gives the right loci and doesn't say whose slip it is. Foot p111–112 has no variant at the numbers.
3. **I07b-1.** The letter is right, but the sentence Wyclif quotes "from Jerome" with John 8:56 is not in *Cogitis me*. Decide whether the quotation marks around it should stay.
4. **I07b-14.** The "Father alone" exposition is Maximinus's, as in waves 1–2. Augustine's own *De Trin.* I.6.10 reads the verse of the Trinity. Wyclif may be setting the Arian reading beside Augustine's answer.
5. **I07b-4** rests on a late-medieval non-Sarum litany (Burnet Psalter). The Sarum Processional was not seen. It could be checked in Wordsworth/Procter.
6. **I07b-6, I07c-1, I07a-3** are left untraced with "perhaps" loci: *Tract.* 45.9; Ep. 140.12; and no hit for Wis. 7:24.
7. Not my row: foot p113 n. 1 (*numerum om. A B C; 10 O M*) at "Ioh. 14" in 07-b.

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **09, p. 17**: *Sed principes sacerdotum et Pharisei tempore legis gracie habent scriptas bullas*, rendered "Now". It is either (a), the next step of the comparison with John 11, or (b), a contrast (they only spoke, ours have written bulls). "Now" is defensible.
- **12, p. 23**: *Sed hoc nimis perturbaret rempublicam*, rendered "But". It is either the minor of Falsehood's reductio (a, "Now") or an adversative (b).
- **19, p. 38**: *Si autem approbarent istud cleri dominium*, rendered "Now if". This is the first horn of a dilemma whose second horn is *Si autem … reprobant* ("But if"). It could be (a), the next step, or (c), "And if".
- **20, p. 40**: *Ordinacio autem Christi … excellit omnes alias*, rendered "Now". It is either (a), a minor under "to admit a bad custom and despise a good one is grave sin", or (c), moving on to the three excellences.
- **27, p. 56**: *Christus autem non dimisit dicere veritatem*, rendered "Now". It is either (a), the step behind the a fortiori *quare ergo … Christianus*, or (c).
- **28, p. 58**: *Ipse autem non fundavit tales elemosinas*, rendered "Now". It is either (a), a minor under "Christ is lord of time, almighty and all-knowing", or (b), a reply to Falsehood.

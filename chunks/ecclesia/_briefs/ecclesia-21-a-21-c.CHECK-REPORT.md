## SOURCE ITEMS
A. Augustine, Enchiridion c. 110 (PL 40, col. 283-284; "CAPUT CX." in PL TEI 7406). WORDING: "Quibus autem prosunt, aut ad hoc prosunt, ut sit plena remissio, aut certe ut tolerabilior fiat ipsa damnatio." Bonaventure/Wyclif's "vel prosunt ut plena fiat remissio vel ut tolleracior fiat eis dampnacio" is a paraphrase. Loserth's foot "cap. CX" correct; codd. "Euch. CXII." is the codices' error. ACTION none (Friedberg labels the same text c.109 et seq.).
B. Friedberg index: "Tempus, quod, c. 23 C. XIII. qu. 2"; text C. XXIII "Item Augustinus in Enchiridion [c. 109 et no.]: Tempus, quod inter hominis mortem et ultimam resurrectionem interpositum est..." and it continues to "ut sit plena remissio, aut certe, ut tollerabilior fiat ipsa dampnatio." CONFIRMED (C.13 q.2 c.23 Tempus).
C. Arius's growing punishment: no match in PL (searched Arii/Arrii poena + augeri; Lombard Sent. I d.11 c.2 only has Arius's burst bowels). NOT LOCATED.
D. Lombard IV d.24 (PL 192, col. 901 ff.): c.1 "septem sunt gradus ... capitis nostri Jesu Christi exemplo monstratur, qui omnium officia in semetipso exhibuit"; c.10 "ordo ... signaculum ... Character ergo spiritualis, ubi fit promotio potestatis, ordo vel gradus vocatur." The littera supports the "character = signaculum" and Christ-exemplar points Bonaventure/Wyclif draw on, but nothing there on a threefold configuration (baptism/confirmation/order) or on a character in *minor* orders; those are Bonaventure's. ACTION: "magister ostendit in litera" = c.1/c.10 in substance only; Bonaventure's own reading is "sicut recte ostendit".
E. nulla est redemptio: earliest in the PL TEI is Remigius of Auxerre, Enarrationes in Psalmos (PL 131): "In inferno nulla est redemptio" (also Ps.-Bede, PL 93 col. 950D "quia in inferno nulla est redemptio"; Bruno Carthusianorum, Bernard, Hugh of St Victor etc. later). Impossibile est multorum preces non exaudiri: Ambrosiaster on Romans (PL 17): "multorum preces impossibile est ut non impetrent"; Ps.-Augustine Ad fratres in eremo (PL 40): "impossibile est, ut preces multorum non exaudiantur" (nearest wording). Non bis punit in idipsum: Cassiodorus, Expositio in Psalterium (PL 70): "Non judicabit Dominus bis in idipsum (Nahum 1)" (Old Latin, with Jerome's Vulgate "non consurget duplex tribulatio"); also Canon Apostolorum in Gratian D.81 (Friedberg: "Non iudicabit Deus bis in id ipsum"). Wyclif's "non bis punit in id ipsum" paraphrases this.

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **07-d, p. 200**: *Sed limitatum est nobis pro fugandis heresibus …*, rendered "But it has been assigned to us". This opens a paragraph and turns from the preceding matter to identical vs tropical predication. No *ergo* closes it, so it is not a minor premiss. "Now" for a new step is possible, but "but" for the turn is defensible. Left.
- **07-d, p. 203**: *accidens **autem** est fundamentum et causa alteri sed non subiectum*, rendered "but an accident is a foundation". Read as (b), set against "nothing is the subject of an accident but substance". "And" (c), extending the same point, is defensible. Left.
- **07-e, p. 208**: ***Sed** tercia est visio intellectualis*, rendered "But the third is intellectual vision". This is the third member of the corporeal / imaginary / intellectual division, so a continuative "and" or "now" for the next step could be argued. "But" fits the contrast it draws at once: intellectual vision is pure in the blessed, set against the two lower kinds. Left.

## Latin doubts

- **100.30** · printed *Joh. XI°, 14* · John 10:14 is meant; Loserth's foot: *Codd.: Joh. X ac XIV. Correxi* · **settled on the plate** (n0171, l. 30): the print reads *XI°*, as the bt. Loserth's "correction" introduced the error; the codices' *X* agrees with the Vulgate. The English gives 10:14 and n. 13 gives both readings, as the card asks. No change.
- **side-note 100.11** · "so that our whole mind is to be fixed an Christ's body" · *an* for *on*, an OCR scar or a misprint · not read on the plate; report only (side-notes are not edited in the chunk).

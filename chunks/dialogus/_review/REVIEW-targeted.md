# Targeted review: *Dialogus* chunks 02, 04, 31, epil-a

Reviewer: independent, fresh session, 2026-09-29. Method: sentence by sentence against Pollard's Latin, checked against CARD.md (four Thakkar classes, connective table) and GLOSSARY.md. Sources in the notes were not re-verified. A note is flagged only where it misdescribes the Latin or English beside it. Line numbers marked "c." are placed from the side-note anchors, because the chunk Latin is not lineated.

Classes: 1 terms · 2 connectives · 3 medieval sense · 4 construal, or a note/headnote contradicting the text.

## Verdicts

**02 (3.15–5.11; about 17 sentences).** No errors. Two style breaches (#1, #2): continuative, enumerating *autem* rendered "now", against the card's explicit rule and its worked example. One weak item (#3), a lexical link loosened. Terms, construal of the relatives (*que se … indisponit*), the ablative absolute, and the mood of *reguletur* are all sound. The notes match the text. Isolated.

**04 (7.12–9.10; about 22 sentences).** No errors. Two weak items: an unnoted doubtful construal of *secundum* (#4), and n. 2 dismissing a live alternative too strongly (#5). Connectives are all present and per the card (*enim*, *nam*, *ideo*, *tamen*, the minor-premiss *autem* at 9.4 correctly "now"). *Simpliciter*, *ex fide* and the glossary terms follow the rulings. Clean.

**31 (69.20–73.25; about 50 sentences).** Two errors. #8 is the *idem … ac si* correlative misconstrued, with the note repeating it. #9 is a broken tag that misstates the term. Four weak items: a *sed* minor premiss given as "but", "bound" for "compelled", the ironic speaker assigned to Matthew, and a tense slip. This is the densest chunk, and all the problems are local construal (class 4) apart from one markup slip. Glossary renderings are otherwise exact (*repplicacio* renewal with a C note, *mortificacio*, *legalitas*, *locus a simili* jibe, *divinitas* vs *deitas* kept apart).

**epil-a (86.2–92.30; about 75 sentences).** One error, #21: *non*'s scope reversed, so the English contradicts its own note. Twelve weak items and two style items. There are two recurring patterns. (a) *Sed* in the minor premiss is rendered "but" (#22, #24), matching #6 in 31. (b) Small construal liberties: a dropped dative (#17), unbracketed or narrowing supplements (#18, #20), English and note out of step (#19, #21), and mechanical use of formulas (#14 *ex fide*, #12/#13 *intendere*). Terms tagged from §A–C are rendered per the glossary throughout. The trouble is at the margin: the formula applied where it doesn't fit, and ordinary scholastic verbs (*intendere*) not covered.

**Overall pattern.** Four errors in about 165 sentences, all class 4 (construal), and none of them the gross Thakkar kind (inverted *protestacio*, "dominion", "consequence"). Class 1 is essentially clean apart from the markup slip. There is one systematic class-2 pattern: minor-premiss *sed*/*autem* rendered "but", and, in the other direction, enumerative *autem* rendered "now" (5 instances across 3 chunks). Class-4 slips grow with chunk length and argumentative density: 02 and 04 are clean, 31 and epil-a are not.

## For the coordinator to decide

1. **04-a n2 T note.** Bonaventure's source reads *esse aptitudinale*, so it supports Harris's emendation *aptitudinalis* against the MSS' *habitudinalis*. Whether the T note and the English should now follow the print is an editorial call. Wyclif himself may have written *habitudinalis*, since he has *secundum habitudinem* at 51.7.
2. **04-a n7, 50.11.** Scotus's *duarum naturarum existens significativum* supports A B C *significancium* against the print's *singulatim*. Also consider following A B C there.
3. **04-a n15.** Scotus supports O's *conceptum unum* (not *compositum*) within the passage Harris prints from A B C. Consider a T reading or a note in the English.
4. **03-d n7 (41.8).** Augustine reads *Neque enim homo Christus non dicitur Filius Dei*. The print's *non dixit* is probably a corruption of *dicitur*. That supports the T note's rejection of the print, though A B C's bare *non* is not Augustine's either.
5. **03-d n9.** The lost clause is certain. Decide whether to print it in the English as a supplied clause, or only to note it.
6. **03-e n16.** Wyclif says the Master *non admittit aliquid … esse hominem, nisi tunc componatur ex corpore et anima*, but Lombard d. 22 c. 1 grants *vere erat homo* in death. He denies only that Christ subsisted as man out of soul and flesh joined. Bonaventure (Quaracchi III, 452) says the Master held Christ man *vere et actualiter*. A C note may be wanted that Wyclif narrows the Master's view.
7. **03-d n15.** Wyclif's 17 (O 19) for book 15: perhaps a misread numeral (xv/xvii). No evidence either way.
8. **Harris's index (latinworks03 ll. 23679–23685)** also lists *De Trinitate, 10? 150, 191* and *De Adnichilacione, 76, 78*. That bears on I01b-9 and any later rows citing those works.
9. **Damascene chapter numbers.** I could not check Buytaert's Burgundio numbering. "ca. 49" comes to Wyclif from Scotus, so it is Scotus's number.

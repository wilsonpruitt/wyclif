## SOURCE ITEMS
A. Gregory Hom. 17 (PL 76, TEI 8053): Luke 10:1 pericope ("designavit Dominus et alios septuaginta duos") opens it; quotations at 449/450/451 match PL: "Qui enim locum praedicationis suscipit, mala inferre non debet..." (17.4), "Praedicatori etenim tanta debet in Deo esse fiducia..." (17.5), "Multi autem cum regiminis jura suscipiunt..." (17.4). The claim about a heavenly hierarchy/eternal priesthood is NOT in Hom. 17 (it speaks of mundus sacerdotibus plenus, sacerdotale officium); the 446 citation is only a loose "hint". Keep E19b-1 as "homily right, content loose".

III ("De Abel et Enoch primatum tenentibus"), so Loserth's III is right, MS IV wrong. Text: "Tota enim justitia haec est, virginitas, sacerdotium, et martyrium. Quae triplex justitia in Abel primo fuit..." matches.
C. Located: the same work, De mirabilibus I.3: "Sed huic Abel Dominus Jesus Christus primatum justitiae hominum commisit ... In eo enim quod dicit, A sanguine Abel justi, usque ad sanguinem Zachariae, et non addidit, justi; haereditatem justitiae Abel tenere Zachariam ostendit. Sicut etiam in genealogia Salvatoris scribitur, Jesse autem genuit David regem, David autem rex genuit Salomonem, cui non additur, regem..." Wyclif condenses; attribution to "Augustine" is the same pseudo-Augustine.

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **03-f, p. 64**: *Spiritus autem sanctus … postulat pro nobis gemitibus*, rendered "And". It is either (c), the second term of the dove / Spirit comparison, or (a), the step that applies the dove's moan to the Spirit.
- **04-a ¶2, p. 71**: *prescitus autem in gracia est verum membrum ecclesie*, rendered "and that a foreknown [man] in grace". It is either (c), the second of two coordinated concessions, or (b), "but", contrasting it with the excommunicated predestinate.
- **04-c ¶5, p. 80**: *Testatur autem scriptura quod Deus odit peccatores*, rendered "And". It is either (c), corroboration added, or (a), scripture brought in as a premiss for "he always hates any foreknown".
- **04-d ¶2, p. 82**: *mali autem angeli non erant de civitate diaboli ante peccatum*, rendered "and". It is either (c), continuing the explanation of priority, or (b), contrasting the evil angels with those who stood.
- **04-e ¶2, p. 87** (the gloss): *Sed certum est quod papa errare potest … Ideo nec ipse … est illa ecclesia*, rendered "But". It is either (a), the minor of a syllogism closed by *ideo*, or (b), set against "the pope, who is called the church".
- **05-e ¶2, p. 110**: *Forma autem per se et ab intrinseco constituens*, rendered "And". It is either (c), the second member of a pair (as with *Forma autem … deformitas autem* at 05-c, p. 103), or (b), setting the intrinsic form against the exemplar form *ab extra*.
- **05-e ¶3, p. 111**: *Illa autem prima adhesio … est eiusdem racionis hic et in patria*, rendered "Now". It is either (a), the premiss that settles the doctors' disagreement, or (c).
- **06-b ¶3, p. 122**: *non habet … aliquod membrum diaboli, sed sicut prudens paterfamilias non adoptat*, rendered "But" as a new sentence. The *sed* adds a supporting analogy after a negative rather than marking a contrast. "But" is defensible.

Not a connective question, but noticed: 04-f ¶2, p. 91 tags "[bridegroom]{g:sponsa}" (it should be `g:sponsus`). The same ¶ reads "book 11 [read 2]" for Hugh. Neither was touched.

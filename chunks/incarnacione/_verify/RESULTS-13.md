## For the coordinator to decide

1. **I09c-1.** The parallels make *in materia De Trinitate* a topic, so Harris's italics for a title should probably go in the English. A forward reference to Wyclif's own *De Trinitate* cannot be strictly excluded.
2. **I09c-8.** Treating "Ome. 19" as a slip or OCR loss for 119 is an inference. Harris's Index II entry for p. 153 is unreadable in the OCR. Decide whether the note should say "probably".
3. **I09d-2.** Wyclif's "Dist. 6" is either a slip for d. 7 (the *De praed. sanct.* recital) or a reference to the d. 6 Enchiridion recital. The replacement states both.
4. **I09d-3.** The anathemas are those of the Toledo *Regula fidei* (5th c.). This identification is from memory and was not checked; it is left out of the note.
5. **I09c-4.** Wyclif's Jerome for *hominem assumpsit* is attested here only in works falsely attributed to him.
6. **09-c n14.** It carries two replacements, both on unique phrases, applied in order.

This *autem* adds a new premiss: that alternative is possible by absolute power and nothing teaches the opposite. The *ergo* that follows draws the conclusion from it straight away (*quare ergo sic non ponunt?*). This is the look-ahead rule: an *ergo* follows, so the *autem* is "now". ## Doubtful, left as they are

- **03-a, p. 57**: *sensus **autem** interiores iudicant de substanciis materialibus*, rendered "Now the inner senses". This brings in a new premiss (the inner senses judge of substances), and *ideo magis malum foret* draws the conclusion at once, so (a) stands. It could also be read as carrying on from "the senses rendered immune", which would make it (c) "And". Left.
- **03-b, p. 60**: *In natura **autem**, quando unum corpus convertitur*, rendered "Now in nature". This opens a paragraph with the natural case of conversion, and *ideo corpus sic desinens non convertitur* draws the conclusion from it: (a). Read as a turn from the supernatural to the natural case, it could be (b) "But", or (c) "And" as one more consideration. Left.
- **03-b, p. 60**: *sed hoc implicat duo instancia esse inmediata*, rendered "but this implies". This is the absurd consequence that the opponents' position runs into, and no *ergo* follows, so (b) stands. Read as a reductio, it could be argued as (a). Left.
- **03-b, p. 61**: *Alii **autem** stulte ponunt*, rendered "But others". This sets the "others" against the "subtler doctors" of p. 60: (b). Read as one more opinion in a list, it would be (c) "And". Left.
- **03-c, p. 64**: *substancia esset non tanta nec talis, alias **autem** est contrarie se habens*, rendered "and at another time is disposed contrariwise". The two clauses pair two contrary states of the substance. The English gives (c) "and", but "but at another time" (b) would mark the contrast just as well. Left.
- **03-c, p. 64**: *informacionem distinctam que sit quantitas et qualitas, non **autem** ficta accidencia sic linita*, rendered "and not feigned accidents". The English "X, and not Y" carries the contrast, so the *autem* is not lost. "But not" (b) would also do. Left.

## For the coordinator to decide

1. **Wyclif counts the Maximinus works as three books.** In both places checked here, his "book 1" of *Contra Maximinianum* is the *Collatio* (I02b-4 and I02c-5), and his "book 3, c. 22" is *Contra Maximinum* II.22. In wave 1 (I01a-9), "libro primo" was explained as *Contra Maximinum* I.17 repeating the *Collatio*. That still holds, but the three-book count may be the simpler explanation. A convention note might be worth adding.
2. **02-b n3 C note** (*formidaret* or *formidarem*). Augustine's *Ep.* 241.1 says to Pascentius: *numquid non rescripsisti quod voluisti, et nullam calumniam formidasti?* (PL 33, 1051). This is the source of Wyclif's *calumpniam formidaret*. In Augustine, though, it is Pascentius who fears no *calumnia*. That may bear on the A B C first-person reading.
3. **02-b n2 T note**: *De haer.* 55 reads *dissenserunt*, which comes closest to O *disserunt*. Whether to say so in the T note, or to change the print's *differunt*, is an editorial call.
4. **02-b n14 / n9**: the quotation-mark problems (where Augustine's words close) can now be settled from the sources. Ep. 238.13 ends at *omnia in omnibus*. The words *non solum natura … meliori natura* are *Contra Maximinum* II.22.1, so they could be marked as quotation. At 02-d n12, Augustine's words end at *per Christum*, which confirms the English.
5. **02-c n5 C note**: the "stray *et*" is Augustine's (*Verbum Dei et carnem*, the heretics' Christ as the Word plus flesh). The change in column 4 says so, but the C note's "left untranslated" may need rewording.
6. **I02d-4** stands on the likeliest match, not a citation. Downgrade it to untraced if you want only firm identifications.
7. **Old letter numbers** ("Ep. 127", "Ep. 40 ad Dardanum") are not traced to any pre-Maurist numbering. A pre-Maurist Augustine edition (Amerbach or Erasmus) would settle them.

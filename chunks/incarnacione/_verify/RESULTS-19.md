## For the coordinator

1. **I13a-4.** The note had the Doctor's position backwards. Harris and the source both make him Thomas, who holds the "many men" view. Check whether the English at 218.21 or the gloss on 218.23 / 219.1 needs adjusting.
2. **I13b-1.** In the *Ordinatio*, "neither one man nor many" is a view Scotus reports and rejects, as at I11a-1. Wyclif follows one reading of Scotus that Lychetus records. Do Wyclif's "third opinion" glosses elsewhere need the same caveat?
3. **I13b-2 variant.** At 221.12 the source (*primis substantiis*) supports the print against O's *principiis*.
4. **I13c-6.** Wyclif's missing question number would be "question 2" if he counted d. 6 serially (q. 1 a. 2). Whether to suggest that in the note is your call.
5. **I13c-5.** Anselm's words are *si vellet*, not Wyclif's *si Deus voluerit*. Marked verified in sense only.

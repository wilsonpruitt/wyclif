1. **127.27 *animam* → *hominem*.** Augustine reads *habet hominem* (PL 35, 1553), and Harris's own Notes retract his print: *Since the text was printed I have found that hominem is the word in Aug.* The comparison also needs it: *Verbum habens hominem* and the closing *Verbum Dei habens hominem*. I recommend reading *hominem* and changing the English and the note to match. The n5 replacement above still says "Translated as printed". Replace it if you emend.
2. **127.23–24 *Filio Dei*** is confirmed, so n4 stands with the replacement. **127.11 *Quantitate*** is confirmed, so n2 stands.
3. **I08b-7.** In the English of 129.31ff., *Quis alius … procreatus* should not be printed as a quotation of Anselm's words. It is Wyclif's question. The doctrine is c. 11 of Schmitt, Migne's c. VI.
4. **Richard (I08c-2, I08d-3).** The "rational nature" form of the definition is not in Richard. It is the schools' generalisation, perhaps Scotus's *intellectualis naturae incommunicabilis existentia* (not checked).
5. **I08b-5 and I08d-4** stay open. They need Dales–Gieben (Grosseteste, *Hexaemeron*, sixth day) and a readable Juntine IV (Averroes, *In Phys.* VIII comm. 46/49).

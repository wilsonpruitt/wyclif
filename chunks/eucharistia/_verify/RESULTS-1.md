## For the coordinator to decide

1. **Kprola-4: the identification was wrong.** The compendium's "decree of Jerome" is D. 95 c. 6 *Ecce ego dico*, not *De cons.* D. 2 c. 49. Prol-a n9 also says "from Jerome (p. 28)". The quotation is at 27.1–7, and p. 28 only takes it up again ("sentencia beati Jeronimi", 28.27). Decide whether to change n9 to "p. 27".

## For the coordinator to decide

1. **"Ome." numbering is not uniform.** "Ome. 47" = tract. 47, but "Ome. 50" = tract. 51. Either Wyclif or his exemplar slips, or his copy divides tractates 50/51 differently. Harris says nothing (foot p030 has readings only). The n11 change records the offset and does not explain it.
2. **I03a-2**: Wyclif names no work, so *Sermo* 377 (PL 39 places it among the *dubii*) is the Augustine text that fits, not a citation. Downgrade it to untraced ("perhaps") if you want only firm identifications.
3. **I03a-5 / I03a-6**: both doctors conclude against Wyclif's thesis that Christ was a man in the triduum (Thomas *Sent.* III d. 22; Scotus *Ord.* III d. 22). Wyclif takes only a concession from each. Consider a C note saying so. Wyclif argues with Scotus on the triduum by name later in this treatise (latinworks03 ll. 5677–5935, with Cowton), so a cross-reference may be wanted.
4. **03-b n11**: Wyclif's 36 hours and *sexto senario* (33.20–34.1) come from the same Augustine book, IV.6.10. The change folds this into n11. The coordinator may prefer to anchor it on its own sentence.

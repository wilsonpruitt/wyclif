## For the coordinator

1. **E13d-2: Loserth is wrong.** Sergius at 290 is the monk of Muhammad, not Pope Sergius III. This also settles how E13d-3's "two false monks" sits: the parallel is between monk-made schisms. The replacement for 13-d n13 is in the table.
2. **E14e-4 bears on two rows outside this group.** Henry's *Quodl.* VI q. 23 (a) is about tithes, so it likely answers E13c-6 ("Henry of Ghent … on tithes", Loserth "vide p. 317"). (b) It gives the *venenum* legend as *legitur … tempore Constantini*. E14e-5 ("not in PL", untraced) should now name Henry as Wyclif's immediate source.
3. **E16d-7: the LEDGER row has the wrong page.** It says "Lechler I 204, 303". The right figure is 294 (an OCR misreading in Loserth's foot); the chunk note already reads 294.
4. **E17b-5: the note overstates.** For this question Souter and PL agree (q. 3), so "numbers the questions differently" should go.
5. **E14b-5: the note's locus is wrong.** "Chs. 8–9" should be chs. 7–11 (148–49, 213).
6. **E15e-1 and E21e-2 are identifications, not proofs.** E15e-1 is left untraced: the editors date *De simonia* after *De ecclesia*. E21e-2 is marked verified with "most likely" kept in the note. Decide whether that hedge is enough.

Decisions for the coordinator:

1. **I12d-4.** "Materia de ydemptificacione" may name a current scholastic debate, not this book's ch. 9. Page 158 is the only place in the volume that states *quidlibet … cuilibet*. Accept the cross-reference, or downgrade it to *cf.*
2. **I12d-3.** In *De comp. hom.* 2, Wyclif cites the *Predicamenta* for exactly this doctrine. *In methaphisica* might mean Aristotle *Metaph.* VII, or his own *Summa de ente*. I did not read *Metaph.* VII.
3. **I12b-1.** Thomas's example is Jesus and Peter. Wyclif's "John, Peter" (208.28) is his own; the print agrees with no source here.
4. **I12c-1.** The n13 English "the whole is greater than the part" can stay. Campanus has no *quantitativa*.

## For the coordinator

1. **P11m-8, the translation.** The print's *cum quibus voluerit* corrupts Augustine's *cum quisque voluerit*. Decide whether to keep "with those with whom one will", or emend to "whenever anyone will" and flag the change in a **T** note.
2. **P11k-5, the quotation marks.** At 11-k ¶5 the translation puts "a frenzied man is to be deprived of his sword and a lethargic man roused from sleep" in quotation marks. The canon says *ligat* ("binds"), not "deprived of his sword", so this is Wyclif's paraphrase. Consider dropping the quotation marks. (Italics and quotation bounds are the editor's call.)
3. **P11l-1.** Loserth's "330—352" against the Rolls Series' 330–32, found in P11c-1. Confirm against the plate before printing a correction.
4. **P11m-2 and P11m-4** still need a source we do not have: a PG 56 scan with cols. 849–859, and a printed *Rosarium* (for example the Venice 1481 or the Lyon 1549 edition, on Google Books or BSB).

- **Rows 3 and 4 together** show that the Families sentence needs a counterweight in practice. Each time the rule's default (cross-family agreement outranks the print) is set aside, the T note should say so in so many words. Without that, the reader cannot tell a weighed decision from an oversight. If the coordinator agrees, a one-line addition to the card's Families sentence would cover it. This chunk alone does not justify a card change, so section (a) is omitted.

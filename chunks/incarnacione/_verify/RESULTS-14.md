## For the coordinator to decide

1. **167.11 (I10b-3).** Augustine reads ***unicus*** at the second place: *Filius Dei unicus esse coepit*. The first *unigenitus* (167.8) is Augustine's too. So O agrees with the source against the print. Decide whether to follow O in the English and say so in the note.
2. **169.2 (I10c-1).** The decretal reads ***de cetero*** "henceforth" (Friedberg II, 779), as B C do. The print's *de ceteris* with A goes against the source. If you adopt B C, a suggested replacement is: 10-c n6: "*De ceteris* \"of the others\" (169.2) is the print with A; B C read *de cetero* \"henceforth\"." → "\"Henceforth\", *de cetero*, with B C and the decretal (Friedberg II, 779), against the print's *de ceteris* \"of the others\" with A (169.2)."
3. **167.4 (I10b-1).** Augustine supports the print: *de quo procedit Spiritus sanctus* (*Ench.* 12.38). The existing note in n9 already says this, so no change is needed. The A B O omission is a scribal one.
4. **I10b-1.** Wyclif's quotation joins *Ench.* 12.38 and 10.35. The replacement says so. *Ench.* 10.35 has *utrumque*, which matches O's *utrumque* against the print's *utraque* (167.4). Consider O's reading there.
5. **I10a-1 / 161.32, 162.4.** In Scotus the three dependences are actual, **aptitudinal** and potential, and the Wadding scholion calls the third *obedientialis*. Wyclif's second dependence (O *potencialis*, A B C *personalis*) matches neither name for Scotus's middle term. Wyclif's third (O alone *obediencialis*) is Scotus's "potential". Note 8, which says "potential" answers *in potencia*, might mention that Scotus's own word is *aptitudinalis*. This was not changed.
6. **I10c-2.** The identification depends on Wyclif's own incipit in *De veritate S. Scripturae*. The DVSS editor's footnote points to "Opp. tom. XVI (De Fide Catholica) 1289". The sermon does not use *univoce*.

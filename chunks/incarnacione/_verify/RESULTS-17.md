## For the coordinator

1. **I11c-3, 197.27.** Yes. *De div. qq. LXXXIII* q. 5 is the source. *Quaest. VNT* q. 5 is on Abel and Cain, so *Vet. et Nov. Test.* is an intrusion and A B C are right. Consider bracketing or dropping the words in the English. The replacement note records only that the source supports A B C.
2. **I12a-1, 202.10.** Gregory reads **utique** (PL 75, 1008C), as O does. Wyclif's sentence already differs from Gregory's (*si utrobique verum diceret*). It is your call whether to adopt O's *utique* ("surely") or keep the print's *hucusque*. If you adopt it, the English and the rest of n2 change with it.
3. **I12a-2.** Wyclif's "q. 8 and 9" is a serial count of the articles in *Scriptum* III d. 1 (4 + 5). The same counting may explain other odd Thomas references in the volume.
4. **I12a-4.** Only Harris's identification stands. If you want the locus, the next step is the edited *De anima* (Dziewicki, *Miscellanea philosophica*?) or Beer's *De compositione hominis*.

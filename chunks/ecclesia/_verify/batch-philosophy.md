## For the coordinator

1. **E20b-4, E17d-9: two small wrong identifications.** *Melior est virtute* is *EN* I.12, not X.6–7. The note's *Porphyry's first sense* is his second. Changes given above.
2. **E16c-3:** *Bolonie* = Boulogne is now established (Raymond m. Marie de Boulogne, Oct. 1375). Decide whether the translation should now say "Boulogne" instead of leaving "Bolonia". The English tithes and the killings are still unchecked.
3. **E16c-7, E21a-1: the notes understated rather than overstated.** The charter narrates the healing itself. The plums are Wyclif's own tale of a deaf old woman, not a proverb. Both notes are rewritten.
4. **E13e-1, E21b-9, E02e-3, E21c-2 stay untraced.** For E21b-9 the Bonaventure source is now verified; only Augustine's alleged saying is missing.
5. **E16h-3, E14h-1, E16c-3 were verified through secondary sources:** a scholarly edition's quotation for Averroes, and reference and encyclopedia sources for the proverb and the Beaufort family. Accept them as "verified", or downgrade them.
6. **E05e-4:** Loserth's *Opp. Aug.* II p. 263 is from an edition we have not identified. The note reports it as his, which is fine.

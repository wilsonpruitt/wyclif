## For the coordinator

1. **K09e-5 against K06h-2.** K06h-2 matched Wyclif's other citation of Rabanus *De sacramento altaris* (at 06-h) to *De clericorum institutione* I.31. Here the matter fits *De sacris ordinibus* c. 19, a commentary on the canon of the mass. The title may cover more than one work, or neither. Decide whether the two notes should say the same thing.
2. **Thomas loci (K09f-1, K09f-5).** Wyclif's question numbers are not the Parma ones (d. 11 *q. 3* is q. 1 a. 3; d. 8 *q. 1* is q. 2 a. 1 qc. 4). I left them as Wyclif's in the text and added the Parma loci in the notes. Loserth's *Opp.* X and *l.c.* pages are from an edition I did not identify.
3. **K09f-4.** Scotus d. 8 q. 2 also reports a *Sententia Richardi* on *hoc* (probably Richard of Middleton). I take Wyclif's *sanctus Richardus, Armachanus* as FitzRalph, as the note does.
4. **K09f-3.** The gloss on *Timorem* stays at editor. Thomas d. 8 q. 2 a. 1 qc. 4 ad 1 independently attests the *materialiter* view, so the note could cite him if wanted.

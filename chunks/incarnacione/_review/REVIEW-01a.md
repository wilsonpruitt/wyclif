## Outside the four classes (process; not counted)

- **Latin intact.** Both chunks' `>` blocks are identical to the brief's scaffold.
- **The brief's apparatus OCR is unreliable at exactly the points that matter.** It loses figures and whole lines: p. 4 nn. 10–11 and 15, p. 6 n. 17, p. 7 n. 3's sigla. The translator mis-keyed one variant to the wrong word (row 32) and missed three readings that bear on the sense (rows 26, 34, 36). In each case the plate foot settled it in one look. The page foot is set in clean type and could be re-OCR'd, or cropped and read per page, at brief-build time.
- **Possibly unchecked identification.** Prol n. 7 (Thomas, *ST* III q. 3 a. 7) states which way Thomas concludes. The ledger row Pprol-3 should confirm the direction of the *ad* 2 (one man or many) before publication, because ch. 12 cites Thomas for "many men" and ch. 13 corrects that.
- **The transcription-notes margin list** has OCR scars ("Jobn", "vni", "[A75d]") and lacks the margin's Gal. 4:4 / Phil. 2:7 if Harris prints them. This is cosmetic, but row 4's headnote sentence depends on knowing which references are Harris's.

## (a) Card changes before the volume runs

1. **Predication line: "*secundum deitatem / humanitatem* 'as to his deity / humanity'"** contradicts GLOSSARY (*deitas* "the Godhead", *humanitas* "the humanity"). The translator rightly followed the glossary in the chunk, but the card's wording leaked into the headnote ("as to his humanity", row 5). Change it to "as to the Godhead / as to the humanity". Add: ***secundum quod* (reduplicative) "insofar as", never "in that"** (row 30). Also add: **never supply "as God / as man" where the Latin has bare apposition** (*ipse Deus*) **or a bare predication**, because the difference between the bare and the reduplicated form is the matter of the treatise (row 33).
2. **Formulae of the disputation.** The card says they are "GLOSSARY §C, ruled 2026-10-01", but *supponitur*, *patet*, *probatur* and *confirmatur* have no rows, so they cannot be tagged, and the translator wrote "It is confirmed" (row 19). Either add the rows or say "formulae: fixed renderings, untagged". Add one line: ***ex fide* is the formula only when no verb governs *ex*** (*ut ex fide supponitur* → "as is supposed from faith", row 18). The card repeats the formula, but not its exception.
3. **Citation formulae** (*ubi supra / ubi super* "in the place cited above", *ut supra*, *infra*): add a line. Row 28 turned *ubi super* into a claim about where the passage stands. Add ***utrobique* "on both sides / in both places", not "everywhere"** to the connective table or §D (row 35).
4. **G notes for newly ruled rows.** The six 2026-10-22 rows are now in GLOSSARY. Say explicitly that from this chunk on they are **tag only**, with a G note only for something specific to the passage. Prol notes 4 and 6 repeat the rows (rows 9–10). The prologue ran at 1 note per 40 words.
5. **Apparatus discipline.** Add to "Never alter the Latin": (i) **a variant must be matched to its figure in the Latin before it is cited**, and where the brief's OCR of the foot is broken at that figure, read the plate foot, which is a cheap, legitimate plate look (row 32); (ii) **abbreviated readings at the foot are rejected readings to be expanded** (*ppcones* = *proposiciones*), not "beyond certain reading" (row 26); (iii) **where Harris prints one manuscript (usually O, or M) against A B C on a word the argument turns on, write a T note**, even though the print is kept (rows 27, 36). The card's own warning (suspect the editor's choice among variants) needs this mechanism to be acted on.
6. **Supplied words**: "in square brackets, never in italics". Row 25 used italics and said so in the note.

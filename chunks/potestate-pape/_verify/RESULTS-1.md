## For the coordinator to decide

1. **P01a-1.** No chapter 14 fits. Should the note keep Loserth's emendation (De ver. "throughout") or say plainly that the reference is corrupt? The proposed text does the latter.
2. **P01b-2.** Wyclif's chapter numbers (Retract. I.32, *De spiritu et anima* 26) do not match the PL numbering. Medieval numbering may differ. The proposed note keeps the text's numbers and gives the PL loci.
3. **P01e-1.** The words quoted under *Miramur* are Gratian's dictum, not the canon. The proposed note says so. The ledger's form "c.37" could be changed to "d.p.c. 37".
4. **Untraced, needing outside sources:**
   - FitzRalph's *Summa* (P01a-4, P02c-1): the Paris 1511/12 print might be at BSB or Gallica.
   - Guido de Baysio's *Rosarium* (P02b-5): only MSS on archive.org; incunable prints exist elsewhere.
   - Wyclif's *De universalibus* (P02a-3): Mueller ed., 1985.
5. **02-b n12 (not a row).** Lombard IV d.18 c.6, Wyclif's source for 29.8–14 ("recitatur inferius"), says Christ *Lazarum jam vivificatum obtulit discipulis solvendum*. So Wyclif's "fecit Apostolos ipsum solvere" follows Lombard. The note's claim that the command was "said to the bystanders, not the apostles" should be qualified. The same chapter has *Ponunt … sacerdotes nomen Domini super filios Israel, sed ipse benedicit* (Num 6:27), relevant to n20.
6. **Hus rows.** All folio references ("Opp." 213a–214a, "fol. 217b") remain Loserth's. The parallels themselves were checked in Schaff's English translation, chs. 10–11, not in Latin.
7. **Note numbers.** The note numbers in rows-1.md are stale and should be renumbered in LEDGER.md.

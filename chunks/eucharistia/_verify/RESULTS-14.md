## For the coordinator

1. **K05k-4 has a wrong candidate.** The Robert is Robert of Geneva (Clement VII), not Grosseteste. This also bears on the construal in n14. *Tanquam Roberti discipuli* may describe the friars themselves, as Clementist, rather than the brethren they accuse. Decide.
2. **K05k-1.** The *pena talionis* is the general canon for a failed accuser. Nothing in Gratian or X 5.1–5.2 is specific to heresy, and inquisitorial procedure did not require inscription. Wyclif's *de natura accusacionis heretice* is his application.
3. **K05k-5.** Thomas excepts divine law, so Wyclif's charge goes beyond the article. Decide whether the note should say so more plainly.

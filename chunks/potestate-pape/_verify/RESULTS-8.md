## For the coordinator

1. **P09g-2: the note's claim is wrong.** Gratian D.40 c.2 reads *loco Pauli … gradum Petri*. Loserth's *Petri et Pauli* is his own emendation of the codices' *Petri et Petri*, not the canon's reading. Should the T note say so, or should the English follow the canon's order?
2. **P09g-3: FitzRalph does not say 301 or "pope".** He has *circa annum domini 350* and *caput aliarum*. The text is the interlocutor John's, not Richard's. Should an R note record Wyclif's alterations?
3. **P10b-5: Loserth's "Flor. Hist. I" is wrong.** The volume is II.
4. **P10c-4: Loserth's *Ypodigma* p. 283 should be p. 284.** The pope is Clement VI (1345, Thomas Hatfield). The *Studien* reference was not seen.
5. **P10b-4: the locus is a judgement.** Bernard 4.3.6 is the best match. Bernard has no "hundreds of popes".
6. **P09g-9 stays untraced.** The *Rosarium* is not available.
7. **P10b-1: supports 10-b n3.** *Flores* reads *eradicandum*, which supports Loserth's supplement. The coordinator may want to say so in 10-b n3.

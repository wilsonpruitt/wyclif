## Outside the four classes (process; not counted)

- **Latin intact.** The two plate readings in the transcription notes (187.4, 187.13: dash spacing) agree with the bt files.
- **Ledger I11a-1 (Scotus, *Ord.* III d. 1 q. 1)** is marked verified. Its claim that the singularity-precedes-personality argument and the Eucharist argument are ones Scotus "reports and rejects" sits badly with Wyclif's *probat Doctor quintuplici racione* (186.18–20). One of the two (Wyclif or the note) misattributes, and someone should look again at n. 7 of the third article before publication (row 4).
- **Transcription-notes line** "reference couid be Psome- (188.21)" is OCR noise from the margin, not a reference. It is cosmetic, but it should be deleted or replaced with what the margin actually prints.
- **Note 8**: "which gives the same sense" for Harris's conjecture *connotant* is generous, because "connote" is a precise terminist term and "call up" is not. It is not flagged, since the print (all codices) is rightly kept and the English renders it.

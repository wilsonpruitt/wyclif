## For the coordinator

1. **K01c-2:** Thomas does not support "nor Christ's own eye". *ST* III q. 76 a. 7 ad 2 grants that Christ's eye sees himself under the sacrament. Decide whether n7 should say that Wyclif's "saints" go beyond Thomas.
2. **K01b-1/2:** Wyclif's Augustine is the expanded medieval *Tract.* 26.18 text, not the Maurist one. The *dentibus* gap at 17.27 against 18.10 is real in the print, and Loserth notes no variant on it. Possibly flag it in n12 as a textual point.
3. **K01d-4:** the *ipsum quidem et non ipsum* clause is not Augustine's *Enarr.* It is the canon's *ad Irenaeum* addition.
4. **K01c-1:** the Gregory miracle is still unidentified. Only the Maurist note can settle it, and it is not local.

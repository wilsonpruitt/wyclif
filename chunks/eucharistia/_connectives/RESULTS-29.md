## Doubtful, left as they are

- **pen02, p. 333**: *Cum autem omnia que eveniunt de necessitate evenient et talis confessio prodest pluribus, patet quod talis confessio est necessario facienda*, rendered "And since". Either (c), the next point of the reply, or (b), set against *non est simpliciter necessaria* just before ("not necessary without qualification; but since everything that happens happens of necessity … it must necessarily be made"). "And" is defensible: the *Et iterum, cum …* of the next paragraph continues the same line.
- **pen02, p. 331**: *Sed quia locucio … periculosa …, ideo procedendo securius dicendum*, rendered "But since". The *ideo* closes the *quia* clause inside the sentence, not a syllogism with the *sed* as premiss; the *sed* turns from the objections to the reply. (b) kept.

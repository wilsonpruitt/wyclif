## For the coordinator to decide

1. **01-a n18** (not my row): the source, the *Collatio* 15.13, reads *creaturam*. That supports A B C against Harris/O *creatorem*, and n18's appeal to the psalm should say so. Whether the English should now follow A B C is an editorial call.
2. **Thomas, ch. 12 notes**: Wyclif's "St Thomas: many men" is the *Scriptum* III d.1 q.2 a.5, not ST III q.3 a.7, which says "one man". Any ch. 12/13 note citing the ST for "many men" needs the same correction.
3. **Ep. 187 column**: the local TEI puts §8 at PL 33, 835. The earlier "836" (2026-10-21) may have used a different column convention. Check before applying the 01-a n9 change.
4. **Iprol-1**: the identification De Anima = *De compositione hominis* stays untraced. Decide whether to keep it in the note at all, perhaps citing Thomson's *Latin Writings* if someone can check it.
5. **I01b-9** (Wyclif's *De Trinitate*) can only be closed with Breck's 1962 edition, which is not available here.

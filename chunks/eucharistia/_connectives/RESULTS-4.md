# Connective check: *sed* / *autem*, De eucharistia, wave 4 (chunks 02-c, 02-d, 02-e)

Classes: (a) minor premiss / next step, "now" · (b) adversative, "but" · (c) continuative *autem*, "and";
*non … sed* (incl. *nec … sed*), "but".

## Changes

| chunk | page | Latin phrase | was | now | reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 02-e | 46 | *Intellexit **autem** Bernhardus per manus, oculos et aures personaliter verbum Dei* | Now Bernard understood | And Bernard understood | c: no new premiss and no conclusion drawn from it. Wyclif's thesis is that Bernard's teaching was catholic but his logic is to be set aside. This *autem* explains how the teaching is catholic (he meant the organs figuratively). It extends the step just stated, and the *Sed nos non debemus predicare taliter* that follows takes up the "logic set aside" half. The card reserves "now" for the minor premiss or a new premiss brought to bear, and it says a continuative *autem* is "and", not "now" |

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **02-e, p. 46**: *Credo **autem** quod sentencia beati Bernhardi fuit katholica*, rendered "And I believe". This applies the general rule just laid down (a later doctor holds only insofar as he is founded on scripture) to the case alleged, Bernard. Read that way it is the step that brings the case to bear, (a) "Now". But no *ergo* closes it, and the rule is not used as the major of a syllogism: the sentence gives Wyclif's verdict on Bernard, so (c) "And" is defensible. Left.
- **02-c, p. 38 (*sed replicatur secundo*), 02-d, p. 41 (*Sed tercio replicatur*), 02-e, p. 47 (*Sed tercio principaliter replicatur*)**: all rendered "But … it is rejoined". In 02-c and 02-e an *ergo* follows (*Quare ergo non teneremus*; *ergo in sentencia contradicunt*), but it closes the rejoinder's own argument, not a syllogism whose minor premiss this *sed* brings. The *sed* turns from Wyclif's solution to the opponent's counter: (b). This is the same as *Sed obicitur* in wave 3 (02-b p. 34). The look-ahead rule does not apply. Left.
- **02-c, p. 37**: *Quod **autem** alii dicuntur in medio prandii accipere … hostiam … videtur esse sompnium*, rendered "But that others are said". This sets the "dream" of a host received mid-meal against the Pauline instruction just quoted, so (b). "And" (c) would also do. Left.
- **02-d, p. 41**: *Figurativas **autem** locuciones scripture debemus exponere*, rendered "But the figurative locutions". This qualifies "the whole of scripture is to be admitted as faith" (it is all true, but the figures must be expounded): (b). "And" is possible. Left.

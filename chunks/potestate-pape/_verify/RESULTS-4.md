## For the coordinator

1. **P05f-1.** Wyclif's *Epistola IX* and Loserth's "Opp. II, 85" may mean Ep. 40, under an older numbering, rather than Ep. 82. The humility passage itself is Ep. 82.22. The Maurist column check needs a Maurist tom. II.
2. **P06a-2.** *De benedicta incarnacione* p. 189 says Christ's assuming another nature *est impossibile*, without qualification. Wyclif here adds *verumtamen potuit facillime si voluisset*. The note's gloss, "the conditional, not the absolute, impossibility", is Wyclif's own refinement and is not in the source cited. Decide whether to say so.
3. **P05f-4.** This stays open. A better candidate may lie outside the Society volumes, such as a *De penitencia* section of a work that is not on disk.
4. **P06a-1 and P06b-3.** The ledger's "no reference in Loserth" and "apparatus cut off" are wrong. Loserth gives both references (103.11, 109.21). Update the LEDGER how-column.

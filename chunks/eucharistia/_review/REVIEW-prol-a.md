## (b) GLOSSARY rulings proposed for this volume's term set

Each: Latin · rendering · reason · section. ⭐ marks the ones Wilson must rule; the rest the coordinator can land as delegated rulings.

1. ⭐ ***solvere / dissolvere* (an argument, an objection)** · **both "resolve"** (*solucio* "resolution") · The compendium alternates them with no distinction: *solvit* 1.4, 1.7, 2.5, 2.25, 3.21, *solvendo* 1.10; *dissolvit* 1.5, 2.1, 2.17, 4.8, 4.11, the two verbs even in one sentence for one act (2.1–5, *dissolvit … et solvit*). They are synonyms, not an opposable pair, so the one-rendering rule does not require two English words; and "answer", the chunk's rendering of *solvere*, collides with the card's formulae *respondetur* "the reply is" / *dicitur* "the answer is". **Alternative:** keep the chunk's split, *solvere* "answer" / *dissolvere* "resolve" (n. 1 as written), which lets a reader see which verb Wyclif used at the cost of the collision. · §C, beside the existing rows *solvere (of the union)* "loose" and *solvere (oaths)* "dissolve". The chunk changes either way: under the recommendation, "answers" → "resolves" at 1.7, 2.5, 2.25, 3.21 and n. 1's last sentence goes.
2. ***predicacio ydemptica / tropica*** · "identical / tropical predication" · the treatise's key pair (2.21); "identical" follows the ruled *ydemptitas* rows, and "tropical" is kept distinct from *figurative* "figuratively" (3.26), which Wyclif also uses. Key `predicacio-ydemptica`. · §C, beside *predicacio secundum essenciam / formalis* etc.
3. ⭐ **The mode adverbs, one row each, one English word each:** *sacramentaliter* "sacramentally" · *realiter* "really" · *vere* "truly" · *spiritualiter* "spiritually" · *virtualiter* "virtually" · *figurative* "figuratively" · *tropice* "tropically" · *ydemptice* "identically" · *invisibiliter* "invisibly" (1.21) · *sensibiliter* "perceptibly" (per *sensibilis*) · *corporaliter* "bodily" · *substancialiter* "substantially" · *dimensionaliter* "dimensionally". The card already forbids merging two in one word; the rows make each taggable and fix "really" for *realiter* (never "actually") and "truly" for *vere*. · §C.
4. ***accidens sine subiecto*** · "accident without a subject" · 3.19; the thesis of ch. 3's second part. · §C, beside *accidens* and *accidens absolutum*.
5. ***remanencia* (panis)** · "the remaining (of the bread)"; verb *manere* "remain" (1.21 *manet invisibiliter*) · the name of Wyclif's position in the controversy; rule it before ch. 1 uses it. · §C.

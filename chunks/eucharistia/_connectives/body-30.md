# sed/autem extract, wave 30: chunks pen05 pen06 quest

### pen05 ¶1 · 338.19–339.26  (1 sed/autem)

LATIN: [p. 338] Videtur tangendum leviter ulterius que mala ex confessione tali per homines introducta poterint faciliter promoveri. Unum **autem** malum ad quod diabolus sepe seducit ecclesiam est quod homines non debite credunt in Deum Jesum Christum et sic errant fide qua crederent in Spiritum Sanctum, cum credunt remissionem peccatorum stare regulariter in iudicio prescitorum. Et sic talis confitens est maledictus qui confidit in homine, capiens sepe audaciam ad peccata talia non timendum; et sic non veretur Deum nec timet delictum in proximum et predicacio ac hortacio [p. 339] per Christum et apostolos precepte nimium sunt soporate, cum papa potest credere quod remissio peccatorum est singulariter suo iudicio reservata; et sic potest mercari cum hominibus servando sibi soli delecionem peccati a crimine. Sic optant et confessores proprii quos ipse specialiter limitat, possunt maiores et minores subvertere et servitutem diaboli introducere ac libertatem Christi paulative excludere. Et sic fratres possent mercari symoniace et observanciam mandatorum Dei tam in se quam in suis confessis perdere, ymmo possent sub sigillo confessionis ad sanctimoniales et alias mulieres accedere et sub forma confessionis seratis clausuris ipsas feminas impregnare, cum olfactus mutuus inter virum et feminam in locis absconditis ex cautela dyaboli provocat ad peccatum. Et hinc Yob pepigit fedus cum oculis suis, ut non cogitaret de virgine. Multo magis lubricus non ipsam olfaceret aut tangeret sic solitarie in loco abscondito, ymmo breviter cautela diaboli et istud medium sunt ad omnia genera criminum inductiva, ut ad peccatum diaboli, ut superbiam, invidiam atque iram, cum confessor post confessionem non audet culpam confessam prodere nec confessum suum private vel publice ad virtutem oppositam inclinare; et sic necessitatur a lege Domini Matthei XVIIIᵒ, 15 Si peccaverit in te frater tuus consenciendo crimini declinare.

ENGLISH: It seems we should touch lightly, further, on what evils could easily be promoted by such [confession]{g:confessio} introduced by men.[^1] And one evil to which the devil often seduces the [church]{g:ecclesia} is that men do not duly believe in God, Jesus Christ, and so they err in the faith by which they should believe in the Holy Spirit, since they believe that the [remission]{g:remissio} of sins stands, [as a rule]{g:regulariter}, in the judgement of [the foreknown]{g:prescitus}.[^2] And so such a one confessing is "cursed", the one "that trusteth in man",[^3] often taking boldness not to fear such sins; and so he does not reverence God nor fear an offence against his neighbour, and the preaching and exhortation [339] enjoined by Christ and the apostles are lulled too far asleep, since the [pope]{g:papa} can believe that the remission of sins is reserved to his judgement [alone]{g:singulariter};[^4] and so he can trade with men, keeping to himself alone the [blotting out]{g:delecio} of sin from the crime. So the confessors of their own, too, whom he specially [assigns]{g:limitare}, choose [it]; they can subvert great and small, bring in the [servitude]{g:servitus} of the devil and little by little shut out the freedom of Christ.[^5] And so the [friars]{g:frater} could trade simoniacally and destroy the observance of God's commandments both in themselves and in those who have confessed to them; indeed they could, under the seal of confession, approach nuns and other women and, under the form of confession, behind locked enclosures, get these women with child, since the mutual scent of man and woman in hidden places provokes to sin by the [wile]{g:cautela} of the devil.[^6] And hence Job "made a covenant with his eyes, that he would not think upon a virgin."[^7] Much more should a man prone to slip not scent her or touch her, alone like this in a hidden place; indeed, in short, the wile of the devil and this means are such as lead on to every kind of crime: as to the sin of the devil, as pride, envy and wrath, since the confessor after confession dares not disclose the fault confessed nor incline his penitent, privately or publicly, to the opposite virtue; and so, by consenting to the crime, he is compelled to turn aside from the law of the Lord, Matthew 18:15, "If thy brother shall offend against thee."[^8]

### pen05 ¶2 · 339.27–339.31  (1 sed/autem)

LATIN: [p. 339] Et quantum ad peccata carnalia, notum est quod istud est medium demonum ad introducendum tam gulam quam luxuriam non solum in confessores et confessos **sed** in personas alias per cautelas quas diabolus machinatur.

ENGLISH: And as to [carnal]{g:carnalis} sins, it is well known that this is the demons' means of bringing in both gluttony and lechery, not only into confessors and those confessed but into other persons, through the wiles which the devil contrives.

### pen05 ¶3 · 340.1–340.28  (3 sed/autem)

LATIN: [p. 340] Et quantum ad peccatum mundi sive cupidinem, patet quod sacramentum istud est plenum cupidine, cum confessores tales nedum accipiunt de confessis suis pecuniam **sed** disponunt occulta media per que ipsam vel sibi equivalens accipient in futurum. Et una cautela novella per diabolum et Anglicos recencius hodie practisatur quod primo reservata absolucione notati criminis Romane curie mittuntur bulle quod homines possent domi absolvi, sic tamen quod fideliter calcularent quantum expenderent eundo a patria sua usque ad Romam et econtra. Et illam pecuniam darent personis mediis per quas poterit ipsis confessoribus devenire; et iustificatur per hoc istud graciosum commercium quod caritative laxatur labor peccantis quem haberet ad curiam et econtra. Et tamen hec immensa indulgencia ex plenitudine potestatis summi pontificis est promissa. **Sed** monoculis est satis cognitum quod ista reservacio absolucionis ad curiam est contra caritatis regulas non fundata; est insuper satis cognitum quod sequens mercacio est plena cupidine atque superbia, cum idem esset sic facere et breviter confesso dicere da mihi tantum peccuniam et securabo te quod eris apud Deum absolutus. Et revera sic in sacramento eucaristie regnat ignorancia sicut in isto; **sed** hic expressior infidelis cupiditas est adiuncta. Istam ergo infidelitatem fideles ecclesie Christi detegerent licet pro eius deteccione penaliter paterentur.

ENGLISH: And as to the sin of the world, or cupidity, it is clear that this [sacrament]{g:sacramentum} is full of cupidity,[^9] since such confessors [not only]{g:nedum} take money from those who confess to them but arrange hidden means through which they will receive it, or its equivalent, in future.[^10] And one new wile is [more freshly]{g:recencius} [put into practice]{g:practizare} today by the devil and the English: that first, absolution for a noted crime being reserved to the [Roman curia]{g:curia-romana}, bulls are sent [saying] that men may be [absolved]{g:absolvere} at home, yet on the condition that they faithfully calculate how much they would spend going from their [homeland]{g:patria} to Rome and back. And that money they are to give to intermediate persons through whom it can come to the confessors themselves; and this gracious [trade]{g:commercium} is justified by this, that the sinner's labour, which he would have in going to the curia and back, is charitably relaxed. And yet this immense [indulgence]{g:indulgencie} is promised out of the [plenitude of power]{g:plenitudo-potestatis} of the [highest pontiff]{g:summi-pontifices}. But it is well enough known to the one-eyed that this reservation of absolution to the curia is against the rules of [charity]{g:caritas}, not founded [on them];[^11] it is moreover well enough known that the trading that follows is full of cupidity and pride, since to do so would be the same as to say briefly to the one confessed, "Give me so much money, and I will assure you that you will be absolved before God." And [in truth]{g:revera} ignorance reigns in the sacrament of the eucharist as it does in this one; but here a more express [faithless]{g:infidelis} cupidity is joined to it.[^12] This [unbelief]{g:infidelitas}, therefore, the faithful of Christ's church should [expose]{g:detegere}, though they should suffer [in hardship]{g:penaliter} for exposing it.

### pen06 ¶1 · 340.29–342.10  (5 sed/autem)

LATIN: [p. 340] **Sed** adhuc replicant quidam per exiles argucias que non forent digne memoria, nisi stulticia per diabolum fuit introducta; ideo ut ecclesia caucius caveat de illa intoxicante stulticia; videtur racionabile pro [p. 341] imprudentibus quod iste argucie per ordinem salutentur. Arguitur enim per illud evangelium dictum de decem leprosis: Ite et ostendite vos sacerdotibus. Cum ergo Christus omnia talia dicta dixerat in figura, videtur quod ex hoc dicto figuravit quod leprosi sunt specialiter a sacerdotibus confitendi. Et sic introducitur sacramentalis confessio usitata ex auctoritate evangelii. **Sed** pro solucione istius oportet supponere in sacramento quomodo est sensus quadruplex scripture, scilicet literalis, allegoricus, tropologicus et anagoycus. Literalis **autem** secundum principia Augustini habet summam auctoritatem, alii **autem** sensus nisi de quanto fundantur in sensu isto autentico vel evidencia racionis, quia aliter quilibet posset moralisare vel trahere auctoritatem scripture ad sensum quem vellet habere, sicut fertur quendam fratrem asseruisse quod veritas evangelica sit quecunque veritas per quam communitas salubriter est hortanda; et sic cum quelibet veritas est huiusmodi, quelibet veritas est veritas evangelica. Sic tenendo scripturam in suis limitibus, videtur quod striccius sit loquendum. Concedenda est ergo historia evangelica supradicta et dicendum quod ex ista non infertur fideliter quod homines debent in tempore gracie taliter confiteri. **Sed** hic videtur probabiliter concludi quod sicut in lege veteri leprosi debuerant se sacerdotibus ostendere, ut patet Luc. XVII, sic Christus autor utriusque legis approbat pro suo tempore veterem istam legem et per anterioracionem sanacionis quod ista ostensio sacerdotibus cessare debuit pro tempore legis nove. Et istud figurat reditus Samaritani qui ultra novem iudeos sanatos taliter [p. 342] est regressus. Potest eciam figurare quod spiritualiter leprosi debent currere ad sacerdotes sub lege gracie, et illi debent per verba evangelica ad salubrem cordis contricionem et peccati detestacionem inmundos spiritualiter inclinare et antea saltem naturaliter sunt ex Dei gracia expurgandi. Sicut enim non infertur ex hoc evangelio quod confitendus ferat in manu sua denarium presbitero offerendum, ita non sequitur ex ista historia quod fidelis debeat istam tradicionem hominum confiteri.

ENGLISH: But some still [rejoin]{g:replicatur} with slender [quibbles]{g:argucie} which would not be worthy of remembrance, had not folly been brought in by the devil; therefore, so that the [church]{g:ecclesia} may beware the more cautiously of that poisoning folly, it seems reasonable, for the sake of [341] the imprudent, that these quibbles be greeted in order.[^1] For [it is argued by appeal to]{g:arguitur-per} that gospel spoken of the ten lepers: "Go, shew yourselves to the priests."[^2] Since, therefore, Christ had said all such sayings in [figure]{g:figura}, it seems that by this saying he [figured]{g:figurare} that lepers are specially to be confessed by priests.[^3] And so the customary sacramental [confession]{g:confessio} is introduced from the [authority]{g:auctoritas} of the gospel. But for the [resolution]{g:solucio} of this one must suppose, in the sacrament, how the sense of scripture is fourfold, namely literal, allegorical, tropological and anagogical.[^4] Now the literal, according to the principles of Augustine, has the highest authority, but the other senses [have it] only in so far as they are founded on this [authentic]{g:autenticus} sense or on the [evidence]{g:evidencia} of reason,[^5] because otherwise anyone could moralize, or draw the authority of scripture to whatever sense he wished to have, as a certain [friar]{g:frater} is reported to have asserted that gospel truth is any truth by which the community is [wholesomely]{g:salubriter} to be exhorted; and so, since every truth is of this sort, every truth is gospel truth.[^6] Holding scripture thus within its [limits]{g:limites}, it seems that one must speak more strictly. The gospel history above is therefore to be granted, and it is to be said that from it it is not faithfully inferred that men ought, in the time of grace, to confess in this way.[^7] But here it seems to be [inferred]{g:concludere} [with probability]{g:probabiliter} that, as in the old law lepers had to show themselves to the priests, as is clear from Luke 17, so Christ, author of both laws, approves that old law for its time; and, by the healing's being put first, that this showing to the priests had to cease for the time of the new law.[^8] And this is figured by the return of the Samaritan, who, beyond the nine Jews who were healed, [342] came back in this way.[^9] It can also figure that the spiritually leprous ought to run to the priests under [the law of grace]{g:lex-gracie}, and that these ought, by the words of the gospel, to incline the [spiritually]{g:spiritualiter} unclean to wholesome [contrition]{g:contricio} of heart and detestation of sin; and before that they are, at least [naturally]{g:naturaliter}, to be cleansed by the grace of God.[^10] For just as it is not inferred from this gospel that the one to be confessed should carry in his hand a [penny]{g:denarius} to be offered to the [presbyter]{g:presbiter}, so it does not [follow]{g:sequitur} from this history that the faithful man ought to confess this tradition of men.[^11]

### pen06 ¶3 · 342.19–343.5  (3 sed/autem)

LATIN: [p. 342] **Sed** sic arguentes ab imprudenti similitudine debent primo attendere quando Jesus prius vivificavit Lazarum et fecit eum surgere de sepulcro fetido atque stare. Et tunc replicans figuram ad propositum debet concedere quod Jesus spiritualiter prius vivificat peccato sic mortuos et eos suscitans facit stare. Et postmodum fideles presbiteri debent solvere ligamenta consuetudinum peccandi sancta predicacione, quibus post delecionem peccati per Deum postea sint ligati. **Sed** ista consuetudinis diminucio sive solucio est presbyteris hodie pretermissa, cum nec salubribus exhortacionibus nec sanctis exemplacionibus docent populum, quando illa consuetudo peccandi est propter periculum limitanda. Et istam fidelem sentenciam debent viri apostolici noticie populi declarare et non se ipsos [p. 343] illis institis tamquam quibusdam apparenciis ypocrisis insolubiliter vinculare. Nunc **autem** ligant se ipsos et indisponunt ad proficiendum populo, cum obligant se diabolo propter apparenciam potestatis et lucri et non reverenter dant gloriam Deo suo.

ENGLISH: But those who argue thus from an imprudent likeness ought first to attend to when Jesus first quickened Lazarus and made him rise from the fetid sepulchre and stand. And then, turning the figure back upon the point, one ought to grant that Jesus first spiritually quickens those thus dead in sin and, raising them, makes them stand. And afterwards faithful presbyters ought, by holy preaching, to loose the bands of the [customs]{g:consuetudo} of sinning by which they may still be bound after the [blotting out]{g:delecio} of the sin by God.[^14] But this diminishing or loosing of custom is passed over by presbyters today, since they teach the people neither by wholesome exhortations nor by holy examples when that custom of sinning is to be [limited]{g:limitare} because of the danger.[^15] And this faithful [teaching]{g:sentencia} apostolic men ought to [make clear]{g:declarare} to the people's knowledge, and not bind themselves [343] [inextricably]{g:insolubiliter} with those bands, as with certain appearances of hypocrisy.[^16] But now they bind themselves and make themselves [unfit]{g:indispositus} to profit the people, since they bind themselves over to the devil for the appearance of power and gain, and do not reverently give glory to their God.

### pen06 ¶4 · 343.6–343.13  (2 sed/autem)

LATIN: [p. 343] Et per ista dicta breviter possunt solvi similes argucie per diabolum introducte. Scimus quidem quod consuetudo istorum ypocritarum novorum ordinum remurmurat contra istam sentenciam. **Sed** super omnia vincit veritas racionis. Non sumus **autem** sic desponsati cum ista sentencia quam adhuc credimus esse catholicam quin docto contrario velimus ipsam humiliter dimittere et contrarium constancius confiteri.

ENGLISH: And by these sayings like quibbles brought in by the devil can be briefly resolved. We know indeed that the custom of these hypocrites of the new orders murmurs back against this teaching. But above all the truth of reason "conquers".[^17] Yet we are not so wedded to this teaching, which we still believe to be catholic, that, were the contrary taught us, we would not humbly lay it aside and confess the contrary more steadfastly.[^18]

### quest ¶1 · 347.3–347.19  (2 sed/autem)

LATIN: [p. 347] Quid in natura sua est hoc album et rotundum quod consecratur a sacerdote et quod videmus oculo corporali in manibus sacerdotis post consecracionem, quod frangitur, quod teritur dentibus, et numquid est corpus Christi, et numquid est sacramentum corporis Christi. Post publicacionem fidei scripture ex testimonio veritatis et sui Apostoli dicitur quod sacramentum eucaristie est verus panis in natura sua et cum hoc idem panis est virtute verborum sacramentalium corpus Christi, sicut est simul Deus et homo. Et solucio arguciarum in hac parte est satis facilis. Nec sunt obiectus trutannici oppositi digni memoria. Alii **autem** infideles taciti dicunt quod illa hostia consecrata est signum sensibile. **Sed** tacent infideliter utrum sit corpus Christi vel in natura sua panis, ut dicit Apostolus et sancti concorditer quousque diabolus est solutus.

ENGLISH: What, in its [nature]{g:natura}, is this white and round thing which is consecrated by the priest, and which we see with the bodily eye in the priest's hands after the [consecration]{g:consecracio}, which is broken, which is ground by the teeth; and is it [the body of Christ]{g:corpus-christi}, and is it the [sacrament]{g:sacramentum} of the body of Christ?[^1] After the publishing of the faith of scripture from the testimony of the Truth and of his Apostle, [the answer is]{g:dicitur} that the sacrament of the eucharist is true [bread]{g:panis} in its nature and, with this, the same bread is, by the [power]{g:virtus} of the sacramental words, the body of Christ, just as he is at once God and man.[^2] And the [resolution]{g:solucio} of the [quibbles]{g:argucie} in this matter is easy enough. Nor are the vagabond [objections]{g:obiectus} set against it worthy of remembrance.[^3] And other [unbelievers]{g:infidelis}, keeping silent, say "that consecrated [host]{g:hostia} is a [perceptible]{g:sensibilis} [sign]{g:signum}." But they keep silent, faithlessly, whether it is the body of Christ or in its nature bread, as the Apostle and [the saints]{g:sancti} say with one accord until the devil was loosed.[^4]

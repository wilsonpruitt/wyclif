## For the coordinator to decide

1. **10-b translation (E10b-4).** "Which is also recited in the next chapter" is wrong in sense: the canon is quoted in cap. 9 (09-e, 201), the chapter before. Should it be rendered "in the nearest chapter" or "in the preceding chapter"? 10-f, 226.8 (*in fine proximi capituli*) has the same usage and should be rendered consistently.
2. **04-f translation.** The text reads "book 11 [read 2]", but the chunk Latin is *libro II°*. "11" comes from the OCR. Proposed: "book 2".
3. **The poison legend, other rows.** E14e-5, E16d-2 and E21g-6 (other groups; marked untraced or open "not in PL") are closed by Higden, *Polychronicon* IV.26 (Rolls V, 130, *Hodie infusum est venenum in ecclesia Dei*) and by Wyclif's own citation of *Cestrensis libro IV* in *De pot. pape* c. 6 (latinworks19). E16d-2's "commonplace (Jacopo della Lana *diffusum*)" can name Higden as Wyclif's stated source.
4. **11-c, 241.1** (*ut patet collacione tercia*) is the same Nov. 17 c. 7 (*templorum cautela non nocentibus, sed laesis*). Its row belongs to another group.
5. **E08a-6** rests on Wyclif's own cross-reference at 44.22 plus Loserth's "I suppose". Should the note say "probably"? I have written it as identified.
6. **Row addresses.** V-04/V-09/V-10 still point to "ecclesia-01a n32/n25/n26"; the notes are now 01-b n16, n8, n9. E11c-6's PL 82:524B should read 537C.

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **08-b, p. 237**: *Loquamur **autem** de aliquo modo … et tunc oportet dicere*, rendered "And let us speak of some mode". This continues the objector's confirmation in ¶3 (*Confirmatur ex hoc quod aliter infinicies foret omne corpus in loco*) by narrowing what "so many times" means, so continuative "and" (c) fits. "Now" (a) is possible, because the narrowed sense is the step that *et tunc oportet dicere* draws on. But no *ergo* closes it, and the *tunc* clause reads as a consequence spelled out, not as the conclusion of a syllogism. Left.
- **08-c, p. 243**: ***Sed** pro multiplicacione communiter sic arguitur*, rendered "But for multiplication it is commonly argued thus". This opens a paragraph and turns from Wyclif's reductio to the opponents' common argument, so "but" (b) fits. Left.

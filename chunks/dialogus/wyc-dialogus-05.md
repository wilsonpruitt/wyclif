# *Dialogus*, chapter 5: the third law: Ezekiel 44

## Truth · 9.12–11.3

> [p. 9] VERITAS. Tercia autem lex veteris testamenti sequitur Ezekiel 44° cap° in hiis verbis. Ait dominus deus, non erit sacerdotibus hereditas; ego hereditas eorum. Et possessionem non dabitis eis in israel; ego enim possessio eorum. Victimam et pro peccato et pro delicto ipsi comedent et omne votum in israel ipsorum erit, et primitiva omnium pecorum progenitorum et omnia libamenta ex omnibus que offeruntur sacerdotum erunt et primitiva ciborum vestrorum dabitis sacerdoti, ut reponat benediccionem domui sue. Omne morticinum et captum a bestia de avibus et de pecoribus non comedent sacerdotes. Talia sunt multa mandata domini, ut patet illi qui in hiis tribus voluerit fideliter quietari. In istis autem tanguntur multe ceremonie, sed omnes ipse sunt cum basali prevaricancia ad sensum alium duplicate. Et cum Christus ex fide sit optimus legis interpres, [p. 10] patet quod sicut ipse qui non venit legem solvere sed implere docuit ipsam debere servari tam opere quam sermone, sic servanda est a sacerdotibus legis gracie, nec capit dictum istud excusacionem, nisi istam nephariam, quod sacerdotes isti quos vides in mandatum istud dei offendere non sunt sacerdotes Christi vel pars ejus; ideo ipsis non attinent iste leges, qui pocius sunt sacerdotes baal vel belial; ideo debent sequi antichristum et per consequens in vita adversari domino jhu Christo. Et sic, si lex domini debet credi, patet quod tales prevaricatores cum suis fautoribus sunt heretici manifesti. Nec credimus [quod] antichristus dispensavit cum istis legibus vel suspendit ipsas quamdiu sibi libuerat. Patet quod ista verba sunt frivola et in die finalis judicii condempnanda. Cum enim tanta sit racio vel major observancie harum legum, modo invalescente cupidine sicut fuit tempore veteris testamenti, patet quod necesse foret sacerdotes modo servare has leges contra avariciam tam sui quam populi, sicut fuit tempore quo non tantum candebat cupiditas. Et videtur peccatum istud invalescere ex cautela speciali diaboli invidentis Christo, qui in temptacione tercia renuit taliter dominari. Ex hoc enim symonia forcius est intrata, parsimonia est suspensa, et evangelizacio cum aliis ministeriis injunctis a Christo est a diabolo istis sacerdotibus interdicta. Per hoc autem inhabiles aspirant ad superiores gradus sacerdocii et impediunt verbum dei predicari in populo; et anelantes ad illud tanquam hereticos persecuntur, et ista persecucio est heresis sue manifestum indicium; [p. 11] nec sufficiunt pauperes et pauci fideles sacerdotes resistere, nisi deus per seculare brachium vel aliunde cicius manus apposuerit adjutrices.

TRUTH. Now the third law of the Old Testament follows, in Ezekiel, chapter 44, in these words:
"Saith the Lord God: The [priests]{g:sacerdos} shall have no inheritance; I am their inheritance.
Neither shall you give them any possession in Israel, for I am their possession. They shall eat the
victim both for sin and for trespass: and every vowed thing in Israel shall be theirs. And the
firstfruits of all the firstborn of the cattle, and all the libations of all things that are
offered, shall be the priests': and you shall give the firstfruits of your meats to the priest, that
he may return a blessing upon his house. The priests shall not eat of any thing that is dead of
itself or caught by a beast, whether it be fowl or cattle."[^1] There are many such commandments of
the Lord, as is clear to him who will rest faithfully in these three. Now in these many ceremonies
are touched on; but all of them, their base being passed beyond, are doubled into another sense.[^2]
And since Christ, as faith teaches, is the best interpreter of the law, [10] it is clear that, as he
who came not to destroy the law but to fulfil it taught that it ought to be kept as much in deed as
in word, so it is to be kept by the priests of the law of grace;[^3] nor does this saying admit any
excuse but this wicked one: that these priests whom you see offend against this commandment of God
are not priests of Christ or part of him; therefore these laws do not concern them, since they are
rather priests of Baal or Belial; therefore they are bound to follow Antichrist, and consequently to
oppose the Lord Jesus Christ in their life.[^4] And so, if the Lord's law is to be believed, it is
clear that such transgressors, with their abettors, are manifest heretics. Nor do we believe that
Antichrist has dispensed from these laws, or suspended them for as long as it pleased him.[^5] It is
clear that these words are frivolous, and to be condemned on the day of the final judgement. For
since there is as great a reason, or a greater, for the observance of these laws now, while greed
grows strong, as there was in the time of the Old Testament, it is clear that it would be necessary
for priests now to keep these laws against the avarice both of themselves and of the people, as it
was in the time when greed did not burn so hot. And this sin seems to grow strong from a special
wile of the devil, who envies Christ, who in the third temptation refused to [exercise
lordship]{g:dominari} in this way.[^6] For by this simony has entered the more strongly, frugality is
suspended, and the preaching of the gospel, with the other ministries enjoined by Christ, is
forbidden to these priests by the devil. And by this the unqualified aspire to the higher grades of
the priesthood and hinder the word of God from being preached among the people; and those who long
for it they persecute as heretics, and this persecution is a manifest token of their own heresy;
[11] nor are the poor and few faithful priests sufficient to resist, unless God quickly lays helping
hands to it, through the secular arm or from elsewhere.[^7]

## Pollard's side-notes (PD, verbatim; not our translation)

- 9.13: Temporal possessions are again condemned by Ezekiel.
- 10.1: And Christ said He had not come to destroy the law but to fulfil
- 10.6: The clergy can only plead that they are not Christ's priests but Baal's.
- 10.9: If Christ's law is to be believed, those who tamper with it are heretics.
- 10.12: It is incredible that the law is suspended now when the need of it is greater than ever.
- 10.20: Witness the increase of simony, etc.
- 10.24: So, too, avaricious priests pant for advancement and persecute faithful preachers.

## Notes

[^1]: **S/T.** Ezek. 44:28–31. *Ait dominus deus* is taken from the end of v. 27; *non erit
    sacerdotibus hereditas* stands for the Vulgate's *non erit autem eis hereditas*; *domui sue* for
    *domui tuae* ("upon thy house"). At 9.17 Pollard prints *omnium pecorum progenitorum*, which does
    not construe ("of all the cattle of the forebears"). B reads *primogenitorum*, "the firstborn of
    all the cattle", close to the Vulgate's *primitiva omnium primogenitorum*, and we translate B's
    reading (CONVENTIONS §5). The rest follows DR.

[^2]: **C.** *Omnes ipse sunt cum basali prevaricancia ad sensum alium duplicate*: obscure. We take
    *prevaricancia* in its root sense, "going beyond" (below, *prevaricatores* are
    "transgressors"), and *basalis* of the literal base: the ceremonies are doubled into a second,
    spiritual sense and are no longer kept in the letter. B, B¹, C read *in* for *cum*. The
    construal is doubtful.

[^3]: **S.** Matt. 5:17, paraphrased: "I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil". *Legis gracie*: "of
    the law of grace", the new law.

[^4]: **C/T.** The wicked "excuse" runs to *domino jhu Christo*: its own premiss, that these are not
    Christ's priests, convicts them. At 10.7 B, B¹, C read *quin* for *qui*: "nay rather, they are
    priests of Baal or Belial". The print's *qui* is kept.

[^5]: **T.** *[quod]*: Pollard's brackets, supplied from B, B¹, C. The same MSS read *Numquid* for
    *Nec* ("Do we believe…?") and the futures *dispensabit … suspendet*. *Dispensare cum*: "to
    dispense from", the term for a papal dispensation.

[^6]: **S.** Matt. 4:8–10, the third temptation in Matthew's order: the devil shows Christ "all the
    kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them", and Christ refuses.

[^7]: **R.** *Pauperes et pauci fideles sacerdotes*: the phrase anticipates the "poor priests",
    the preachers associated with Wyclif (LEDGER D05-1). *Per seculare brachium*: the secular arm,
    which in canon law carried out the church's sentences on heretics, is here asked to help the
    faithful priests (LEDGER D05-2).

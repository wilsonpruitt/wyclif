## For the coordinator

1. **E11a-5, wrong pope.** The Westminster bull is by Nicholas II, not Nicholas III. Wyclif's text, the B margin and Loserth's foot at 206.26 all say "III". Decide whether to flag this in the translation as well as the note. A Wyclif slip should not be emended in the Latin.

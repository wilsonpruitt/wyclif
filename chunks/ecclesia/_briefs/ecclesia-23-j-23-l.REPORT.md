# Report: ecclesia 23-j–23-l (end of cap. 23 and of the treatise)

All three chunks are written with `status: translated`. A diff against the brief's scaffolds shows the Latin unchanged. Lint: 0 problems. bible.py checked every scripture reference in one pass before writing: Rom 1:14, Matt 6:12, Matt 16:19, Job 13:7, 1 Cor 15:14, 2 Cor 2:10, Judith 8:13, Matt 25:25 and Matt 10:36.

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **09-n, p. 325**: *Cum autem bonum hoc putridum sit causa bellorum, patet quod presbiteri … sunt causa*, rendered "Now since". Either (a), the premiss that brings the conclusion *patet* (rotten good causes wars, so priests cause wars), or (c), a further remark after *Taceo … et voco temporalia*. "Now" is defensible: it is the premiss the *patet* draws on.
- **pen01, p. 329**: *Talia autem ridicula infinita que fideles evacuant*, rendered "And such are". Either (c), closing the list of absurdities, or (b), the faithful set against Antichrist's doctrine ("But the faithful do away with such absurdities"). "And" kept, since the clause sums up what precedes.
- **pen01, p. 330**: *In inpossibiles autem errores incidunt … de penitencia*, rendered "And". Continuative, passing to the second of the two sacraments named at the start; a transition, not a premiss, so not "now".

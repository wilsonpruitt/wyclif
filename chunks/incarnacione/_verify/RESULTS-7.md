## For the coordinator to decide

1. **78.23 *inconsequens* → *inconveniens*?** Anselm's word, in the very chapter Wyclif cites and on the same question, is *inconveniens*, and O M read it. Harris says it *should have been in the text*. The case for emending is strong. If you emend, n11 becomes a departure note, and the translation's *inconsistency* (with its gloss g:inconsequens) needs changing to *absurdity* / g:inconveniens.
2. **79.11 *sicut filius insinuat*.** The source confirms the departure already made in n17. No change is needed beyond the locus.
3. **Wyclif's chapter numbers for Anselm (6, 7, 9) and for Augustine (Enchir. "3" = 40, "14" = 22; *De vera rel.* "27" = 16.30)** match no modern or Migne division. The notes record them as Wyclif's and do not explain them.
4. **I05d-4** is left untraced. *Contra Maximinum* II.12.3 is a "perhaps". Decide whether to accept it.
5. **I05e-4 Thersites.** Wyclif's own parallel use in *De apostasia* confirms the reading as Thersites, against Harris's "logical fallacy". The literary source (Ovid / Juvenal / *Ilias Latina*) is not checked.
6. **I06a-5 Grosseteste** needs Dales–Gieben (1982) in hand.

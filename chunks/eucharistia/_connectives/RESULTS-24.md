# Connective check: *sed* / *autem*, De eucharistia, extract 24 (chunks 09-a – 09-c)

Classes: (a) minor premiss / next step, "now" · (b) adversative, "but" · (c) continuative *autem*, "and";
*non … sed* (incl. *nedum / non solum … sed*), "but".

## Changes

| chunk | page | Latin phrase | was | now | reason |

| 09-b | 277 | *cum testificacio non sit maior quam quatuor magnorum doctorum …, illi autem prohibuerunt credi dictis suis … que ergo racio credendi* | teaches, and they forbade | teaches; now they forbade | a: minor premiss (major: no testimony greater than the four doctors'; *autem*: they forbade belief beyond scripture/reason; *ergo*). *prohibuerunt* is indicative, so it stands outside the *cum* clause; the English folded it into "since … and" and lost the step |

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **09-c ¶1, p. 281**: *Sed Deus non tardaret revelare fidem istam*, rendered "But". Either (b), set against "unless perhaps it is made clear by revelation", or (a), the premiss that God would not have delayed the revelation (so none has been given). No *ergo* follows; "But" is defensible.
- **09-c ¶4, p. 282**: *In testem autem veritatis non debet accipi nisi qui habuerit eius noticiam*, rendered "And". Either (c), extending the witness step, or (a), the new premiss that leads to *Et hec est racio quare leges ecclesie … plus credunt quatuor sanctis doctoribus*. "And" is defensible.
- **09-c ¶4, p. 283**: *In ambiguis autem extra fidem scripture solent periti silere*, rendered "And". Either (c), or (b), the expert who keep silent set against the law doctors who sow heretical meanings.
- **09-c ¶5, p. 283**: *Iste autem Lanfrancus, licet fuerit sanctus*, rendered "But". Either (b), the censure set against "the chief" doctor, or (c), the next step onto Lanfranc's method ("And this Lanfranc").

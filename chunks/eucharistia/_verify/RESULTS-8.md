## For the coordinator

1. **04-c n6, *Sinodus* (91.28–29).** Gratian's rubric for D. 16 c. 6 is *Sexta synodus canones conscripsit*. That supports reading *Sinodus* with the D. 16 reference, as the canon's matter (the sixth synod), rather than as the opening of the next one. Decide whether n6 should say so.
2. **K04b-3.** *Tract.* 13 has no literal *hoc est Christus*. The 13.5 passage is the nearest fit, and Wyclif's *videtur* is loose. Keep it as verified with the hedged note, or downgrade it to editor.
3. **K04b-4.** *Lucas in Siro* is not in FitzRalph IX.1 (he has John in Greek). It may be Wyclif's slip or a different source. Possibly worth a T/C flag in n12 (the proposed text already says so).
4. **K04d-1, ABCD *Omelia XXVIII*.** Not resolved. Possibly another homily (Migne hom. 28 is not checked).

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **18-a, p. 417**: *Quamvis autem predestinati deficiunt a fide …, habent tamen fidem infusam*, rendered "And although". (c), or (a), the premiss brought to bear before *Si ergo secundum doctorem … multo magis*.
- **18-a, p. 417**: *ponit ecclesiam triumphantem esse sine macula vel ruga, triumphans autem est immunis ab omni culpa*, rendered "but the triumphant is immune". The contrast (with the militant) is unexpressed in the Latin; (b) as rendered, or (c) "and the triumphant".
- **18-e, p. 433**: *Dicitur autem sancta mater ecclesia proprie senescere*, rendered "And holy mother church is said". (c), or (b), correcting the doctor ("old woman" fits the synagogue of Satan, *but* holy mother church properly grows old in virtue).
- **19-a, p. 442**: *Est autem inhabilis, quia in mortali peccato …*, rendered "And he is unqualified". (c), or (a), the premiss (the foreknown is unqualified) that the major *Deus … non daret potestatem inhabili* needs.
- **19-b, p. 447**: *ipse autem non fuit inhabilis ad recipiendum illam potestatem*, rendered "and he was not [unqualified]". (c), or (a)/(b): it answers the earlier argument by denying its minor.
- **19-d, p. 456**: *secundum decretum IXª, q. I. Sed excommunicati*, rendered as the canon's incipit *Sed excommunicati* (per LEDGER E19d-3); not a connective of Wyclif's sentence, so not rendered as one.
- **19-f, p. 463**: *Suppono autem de logica quod sicut confundit*, rendered "Now I suppose from logic". (a), the new premiss that refutes the porism, or (c).
- **19-g, p. 466**: *Cras tu et filii tui mecum eritis. Saul autem et filii eius creduntur fuisse reprobi*, rendered "But Saul and his sons". (b), or (a), the premiss completing Augustine's third reason.

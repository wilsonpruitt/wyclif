# Connective check: *sed* / *autem*, De ecclesia, extract 06 (chunks 15-h – 17-f)

Classes: (a) minor premiss / next step, "now" · (b) adversative, "but" · (c) continuative *autem*, "and";
*non … sed* (incl. *nedum / non solum … sed*), "but".

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **16-a, p. 363**: *Sed alii multi sancti post et ante in sentenciam illam concordant*, rendered "Now". Either (a), a further step of the reply, or (c), "And many other saints"; the following *sed (ut reor)* is clearly (b).
- **16-b, p. 363**: *Minor autem non probatur*, rendered "Now the minor". The reply turns from the major to the minor: (a) next step, or (c) "And the minor".
- **16-e, p. 372**: *sed et usque hodie … remanet illa vita*, rendered "and even to this day". *sed et* is the additive idiom ("and moreover"); "and even" carries it.
- **16-f, p. 379** (Hugh, quoted): *Laicis vero … terrena possidere conceditur, sed clericis spiritualia committuntur*, rendered "But to lay … whereas to clerics". "But" is spent on *vero*; "whereas" carries the adversative *sed*.
- **16-f, p. 380**: *non usus fuit illa potestate, sed et Augustinus rogavit*, rendered "; and Augustine also asked". Additive *sed et*, carried by "and … also"; "but Augustine too" is the stricter form.
- **17-a, p. 388**: *Assumpsi autem in primo argumento*, rendered "Now in the first argument I assumed". Opens a new section returning to the earlier assumption: (a) next step, or (c).
- **17-b, p. 391**: *fuit autem caput ecclesie antequam fuit homo*, rendered "and he was head". (c), or (a), the premiss behind *Ideo foret nephandum sophisma*.
- **17-c, p. 395**: *Michi autem omnium sanctorum minimo* (Eph 3:8), rendered "To me, the least of all the saints" with no word for *autem*. This keeps the Douay wording, which also leaves it out. It is the only scripture quotation in this extract where the connective goes unrendered.
- **17-d, p. 399**: *sed nec secundum deitatem est Christus pars ecclesie*, rendered "but neither". (b), or (a), the premiss that denies both members of the supposed two natures.
- **17-f, p. 407**: *Illud autem genus fidei habet unam partem quantitativam*, rendered "Now". (a), the step answering the doctor's evidence, or (c).
- **17-f, p. 410**: *de secunda vero ecclesia cadunt predestinati …, sed presciti quandoque cadunt simpliciter*, rendered "but from the second church … whereas the foreknown". "But" is spent on *vero*; "whereas" carries *sed*.

Noticed in passing (not a connective): 17-b, p. 393, "there is now [the church triumphant]{g:ecclesia militans}" carries the wrong tag key (the Latin is *ecclesia triumphans*).

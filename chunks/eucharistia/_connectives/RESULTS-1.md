## Doubtful, left as they are

- **01-a, p. 15**: *Sed quis tunc audiret missam …?*, rendered "But". The sentence still belongs to the third objection: either (a), its last step (the priestly power grows cheap; *now* who would then hear mass …?), or (b), the objectors' indignant turn against the consequence they have drawn. No *ergo* follows inside the paragraph; "But" is defensible.
- **01-a, p. 13**: *Sed hic obiciunt quidam de nostris*, rendered "But". A new objection after the replies, so it could be (a), the next step; it is also set against the replies just given (b). Left.
- Out of scope but noted: **prol-b, p. 7**, *ymmo inconvenientis multiplicis implicacio* is rendered "but rather the implication". The card gives *ymmo* as "indeed, rather"; the "but" comes from the *non est … ymmo* construction, not from a *sed*. The *sed* in the same sentence is rightly "now".

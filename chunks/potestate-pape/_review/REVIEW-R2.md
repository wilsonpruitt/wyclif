## Latin doubts (for the plates)

- **c. 162.21 (07-h):** *diffidenciam deChristo*: the space is lost (*de Christo*). OCR scar in the established text; the English is unaffected.
- **c. 163.26 (07-h):** *ditacio nonpotest attingere*: the space is lost (*non potest*). Scar; English unaffected.
- **c. 167.28 (08-a):** *cum fit eorum dominus et curatus*: causal *cum* would take *sit*; *fit* may be an OCR or print slip for *sit*. The English "since he is made" depends on *fit*; with *sit* it would be "since he is their lord and curate". Check the plate.
- **side-note 121.29 (06-e):** "Christ chose poor simple men for his disciples; …" lacks the numeral "4." that the side-notes before and after carry (1., 2., 3., 5., 6.). Probably dropped in the side-note OCR; check against the plate.

## Verdicts

- **06-e (118.6–122.29):** clean. Connectives, scripture and the eleven-conditions syntax all hold; only tagging and note housekeeping (a CONTEXTUAL note missing, a wrong row cited, two notes describing OCR scars as "the print").
- **07-c (142.13–146.33):** sound. One weak construal (*ex calore fidei … satis salvum*, #9); the rest is first-occurrence tagging and a note that misstates why a verb is supplied.
- **07-h (160.5–164.32):** sound but with three weak items, all in places where a referent is chosen silently or a §D ruling is misapplied: the unexpressed subject of *superabit*, Christ as *bonus dominus cesar*, and *ingratam* against the chunk's own "ungratefully".

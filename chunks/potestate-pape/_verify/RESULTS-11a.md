## For the coordinator

1. **P11d-1, the translation.** FitzRalph reads *quo ad potestatem*. The 11-d n1 sentence is now rendered "as a conclusion, as regards the power". Decide whether to retranslate it as "that, as regards the power …" (keeping *conclusio* as a flagged corruption) or to emend. The replacement note above records the reading but makes no claim about the rendering.
2. **P11d-1, the chapter.** Wyclif's "end of chapter 5" does not match the 1512 print, where the passage is in ch. 6. Possibly Wyclif's MS divided the chapters differently. The note now says only what the print shows.
3. **11-d n4, not in my rows.** The Old Testament run at 275.25 ff. (Lev. 15, Deut. 23, "Ezek. 45", Exod. 30, the vestments of Exod. 28–29) follows FitzRalph's same column almost point for point. FitzRalph himself prints "ezech. c.45". So the wrong chapter is inherited from FitzRalph, not Wyclif's or Loserth's slip. n4 could say so.
4. **Hus folios (P11c-2, P11c-4).** Hus's folios 219/220 were checked only for content, in Schaff's English translation. Loserth's folio numbers stand as his.
5. **P11b-1.** The note now admits the Aristotle loci are only "nearest". If no better locus turns up, the coordinator may prefer to drop the citations altogether.

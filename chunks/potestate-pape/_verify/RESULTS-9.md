## For the coordinator

1. **P10f-1: the attribution is wrong.** *Si solus Christus* (D.8 c.9) is Cyprian, not Augustine. Loserth does give a foot ("Cap. IX").
2. **P10j-3: Loserth's column "1375" is doubtful.** His own feet put *Sermo* 354.7 at 1378 and 354.9 at 1379. The plate for p. 255 could settle whether the OCR "i375" is really 1379.
3. **Old *De verbis Domini* numbers are not confirmed locally.** It is not shown whether 53 = *Sermo* 354 or 50 = *Sermo* 138. The replacement notes avoid claiming it.
4. **P10h-1 / P10j-2: some details are not in Walsingham.** The months (April, August, September 1378), Anagni, and the Fondi election are standard history. Keep them as stated, or cite a modern reference.
5. **P10i-1 and P10j-1 stay untraced.** The canonists' dates and the "mercenaries" occasion are unresolved. For P10i-1, Schulte II (Loserth's reference) failed to download.
6. **P10j-7: only the occasion is verified.** The sermon text itself was not read.

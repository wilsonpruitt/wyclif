## For the coordinator to decide

1. **Wyclif's misattributions.** For P02e-4 Wyclif says Augustine where Gratian has Cyprian. For P03d-2 he says Gregory where Gratian has Ambrose. For P02e-3 the maxim is Simplicius's, though Gratian has *Idem* (Gregory) at c.63. The new note texts state the facts and leave the translation as it is. Confirm that this is the house treatment.
2. **P03b-6.** Loserth's "II, 62" falls in ch. VII, on faith. I have put Wyclif's ch. VIII at p. 69ff. Accept, or ask for a closer look at ch. VIII to find the Matt. 18:15 passage itself. (Matt. 18:15 appears in that volume at pp. 9 and 120, ch. XI.)
3. **P03b-4, "Sermo IV".** This is still unidentified. The guess that *IV* is a corruption of *XIV* (*Sermo* 75 is, I believe, the old *De verbis Domini* 14) is unconfirmed and is not written into the note.
4. **P03c-7, the prayer *Ad Spiritum Sanctum*.** It was not found in PL. The next places to look are pseudo-Augustinian prayer collections (e.g. Wilmart) or a web search.
5. **P03a-1** is waiting on the 1512 FitzRalph print. **P02d-4** is waiting on PG 56 (*Opus imperfectum*, hom. 22), which is not on disk.
6. **Loserth's columns.** His *Opera* references (V, 415/416; V, 1688; IV, 1207 against III, 2, 820) follow an edition whose columns do not match PL. They are kept as his and were not reconciled.

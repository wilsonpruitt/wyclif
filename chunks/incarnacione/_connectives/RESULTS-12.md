## Doubtful, left as they are

- **08-e, p. 139**: *Sed admiracio ineffabiliter nobis mirabilis est … Ideo hoc oportet nos ex fide supponere*, rendered "Now". Either (a), the premiss closed by *Ideo* (the card's look-ahead rule), or (b), the hypostatic union set against the virgin birth's natural probability just granted ("But the marvel"). "Now" stands under the rule.
- **08-e, p. 138**: *nec est auctenticum nec expertum; sed raciones seminales forent superflue limitate*, rendered "but [otherwise]". The objector's second reason: (b), or a continuative "and [besides]". "But" kept.
- **08-e, p. 142**: *quinarius autem et senarius sunt numeri circulares*, rendered "and". Either (c), a remark extending "circularly", or (a), the premiss that justifies it ("now five and six"). "And" defensible.
- **09-a, p. 143**: *Si autem esset conversio … Si autem esset confusio*, rendered "Now if … And if". The first opens the proof (a), the second extends the same step (c); consistent with the card.
- **09-b, p. 149**: *extra racionem humanitatis, sed esse aliquid est racio per se humanitatis; … ideo*, rendered "now". Look-ahead rule (*ideo*) gives (a); the content is also a contrast (person outside / something inside the account of humanity), so "but" would be defensible.
- **09-b, p. 150**: *tunc concedendum esset quod personam hominis assumpsit: sed assumptum est impossibile*, rendered "but". Destroys the antecedent of a conditional, so arguably (a) "now"; but no *ergo* follows and English idiom for a counterfactual reductio is "but". Kept.
- **09-d, p. 156**: *Secundum non est dandum … Primum autem ex hoc videtur deficere … Christus ergo*, rendered "And the first". The two disjuncts are eliminated in one step before *ergo*, so (c); "Now" would also be arguable.
- **09-c, p. 151** (not bolded in the extract): *Diversio autem⁷ ab istis principiis*, rendered "Therefore". Not an error: a recorded departure, the English following C (*ergo*) and O (*igitur*) against the print, with a T note ([^7]) giving the print's *autem* as "And".

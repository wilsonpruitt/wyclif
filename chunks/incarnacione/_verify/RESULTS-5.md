## For the coordinator to decide

1. **04-d n6, 62.19–20.** Augustine (119.6) supports the print's *Quis … ita* against A B C. The T note may now say so outright.
2. **04-c n9 (59.1).** *Contra Simplicianum* is wrong, and *Contra Maximinum* I.19 fits the sense and the book number. Decide whether to treat it as Wyclif's slip (as here) or a scribal one. Harris's foot (p059) has no variant at the title.
3. **04-c n17 / n15.** Thomas and Bonaventure neither require nor exclude Harris's supplied *[corpus]* at 60.19. The T note's decision to follow the MSS stands on the Latin alone.
4. **04-c n15.** Wyclif says Bonaventure holds it *de substancia fidei*. Bonaventure's text does not say this; Quaracchi's scholion says only that Catholics do not dispute it. Consider a C note that the phrase is Wyclif's.
5. **04-c n20 (I04c-9).** Wyclif's "De Anima" for *GA* may be a slip for *De animalibus*: in the Latin Aristotle (Michael Scot), *GA* is books XV–XIX of *De animalibus*. I did not check this. If adopted, it also bears on I03d-5 and Iprol-1, where Harris's index treats *De Anima* as Wyclif's own.
6. **04-c n2 (I04c-1).** The modern identification of Cowton (and the Snettisham *abbreviatio*) comes from a web search, not from a source I read. It is marked "perhaps" and left untraced.
7. **04-d n4.** Henry discusses the same doctrine in *Quodl.* III and X, but Wyclif's "12°" is right for the question that matches him most closely (XII q. 12).
8. **04-b n12.** The current note says the Lombard "gathers" Augustine and Hugh. Wyclif says only that they wrote before the Master, and the Lombard does not cite Hugh. The replacement removes the overstatement.

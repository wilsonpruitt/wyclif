3. **P09d-1.** The gloss is ps.-Augustine, *De vera et falsa poenitentia* 10.25, which Lombard quotes at IV d.18 c.5. Wyclif's "cap. IX" is unexplained. Should a T line say so?
4. **P09d-2: Loserth's page is wrong.** "Opp. V, 595" is the Maurist *Sermo* 121. The real source is *Tract.* 121.4.
5. **P09d-3: Loserth's number is wrong.** CCCLV should be CCCLIV (354.9).
6. **Sermo 142 underlies the passage.** It supplies P09d-4 and P09d-6, and its §5 (*Tumuerat autem superbia, et ipso tumore per angustum redire non poterat … Magnitudo enim soliditatem habet, tumor inflationem*, PL 38, 780–81) underlies Wyclif's *collisus … tumor … aquositas* (206.29–207.4, 09-d n9). Perhaps add an S note at n9.
7. **P09d-5 is "probable" only.** It is closest to *Tract.* 29.8, but there is no verbal match. Should it be treated as identified or kept tentative?
8. **P09b-1 bears on the text at 198.5.** Higden reads *plus inde saecularis adepta sollicitudinis*. This suggests that the codices' *temporalia* is a corruption of an adjective (*temporalis* = *saecularis*). Loserth's *plus* is a guess. Consider a T line at 09-b n2.
9. **P09b-4.** Higden's MSS C.D. have *jussu Constantii*. Wyclif's *Constantini* is a slip, probably his own or his exemplar's. The English can keep "Constantine" with the note.
10. **P09a-1.** Friedberg's inscription *Idem* after a Gregory canon explains "Gregorius". This may also deserve an R/C line.
11. **P09f-4.** At 212.24 Wyclif has *inferior* where the homily has *justior*. Consider a T/S line in 09-f n9; the change above mentions it.
12. **P09a-3** stays untraced. *De blasphemia* cap. 5 is Wyclif's own parallel.

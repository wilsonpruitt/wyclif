## The volume

*Tractatus de potestate pape*, ed. Johann Loserth (Wyclif Society, 1907), from the plates of archive.org
`latinworks19wycl` (**leaf = page + 61**). Written 1379 (Loserth), after *De ecclesia* and *De veritate
sacre scripture*, both of which it cites ("libri proximi" at 1.7 is the editor's crux: he takes the
nearest book to be *De officio regis*). A **treatise** in twelve chapters: Wyclif speaks in the first
person, states conclusions, raises objections (*instatur*, *obicitur*, *arguitur*) and answers them.
Its subject is power (*potestas*), what it is and how it is divided, then the papal power: Peter's
primacy, the pope as Christ's vicar, the election of a pope, the "Caesarean" pope and the endowment,
and Antichrist. **Richard FitzRalph** (*dominus Ardmacanus*, "the lord of Armagh"; *Summa in quaestionibus
Armenorum*) is a constant partner, quoted and answered. Pp. 398–406 are a medieval **compendium**
(*Sentencia libri … in compendio*), a summary of the chapters that came down with the work: translate it
like the text.

**Sigla** (Loserth): **A** Prague, Metropolitan Chapter C 73 (his base, "well-preserved", a copy made
"by a man of learning and ability"); **B** Prague, University Library III F 11 (early 15th c.; Loserth
argues A and B are closely related and A cannot have been copied from B); **C** Prague Univ. XI E 3,
variants only near the end (the *Protestationes* extract). Both A and B carry marginal notes, which
Loserth prints ("A in marg.: …", "B in marg.: …"); A's copyist was a Catholic reader who annotates
against Wyclif ("Cave … contradicit sibi"). Cite variants with these sigla.

**The page foot** (in your brief as "apparatus", by page) has two blocks, both keyed to the line:
the variants (`27, 28. B: et aliqua potencia est creata deest.`) and Loserth's source references (`27. Decret. Unam sanctam.
Extrav. comm. lib. I, tit. VIII, cap. I.`). The OCR of it is rough: read it for sense, not letter by letter.

## The four error classes (Thakkar) — check every sentence

1. **Terms:** GLOSSARY.md governs, one term one rendering. Tag the **first occurrence in the chunk**
   of every §A–C term: `[quiddity]{g:quiditas}`. The key must exist in GLOSSARY.md. A term that looks
   technical but has no row: render it sensibly, untagged, and propose it in your report.
   **CONTEXTUAL** rows hold two senses English splits: choose by context and write a **C** note at
   the first occurrence in the chunk and wherever it leans to the other sense (the dikaiosune rule,
   CONVENTIONS §6).
   Key rulings carried from *De ecclesia*: *ecclesia* church (**lower-case**) · *caput* head ·
   *membrum* member · *predestinati* the predestinate · *presciti* the foreknown · *papa* pope ·
   *vicarius Christi* vicar of Christ · *caritas* charity (never love) · *dominium* lordship · *viator*
   wayfarer · *ex fide* as faith teaches · *sentencia* teaching (the rest: GLOSSARY).
   ⚠ **This volume's own term is *potestas*** and its kin (*potencia*, *auctoritas*, *iurisdiccio*,
   *primatus*, *dotacio*, *papa cesareus*): check GLOSSARY for each; where it has no row, render
   consistently within your brief and PROPOSE the row (the coordinator rules between briefs).
   **Ruled (Wilson, 2026-10-07):** *potestas* is always "power". *potencia* is CONTEXTUAL: "potency" inside
   Aristotle's analysis of *dynamis* (active / passive potency, rational potency, the potency terminated),
   "power" elsewhere (God's power, free choice, ordinary might); *potencia absoluta* stays "absolute power".
   Wyclif uses the two words interchangeably (*potestas vel potencia* 4.7, *potestatem sive potenciam* 4.11):
   render such a pair "power or potency" and write a **C** note at the first one in your chunk. Never write a
   note or headnote that claims the two words differ in sense; ch. 1 shows they do not.
2. **Connectives carry the argument.** ⛔ Never omit one, never turn one into filler.

   | Latin | English | Never |
   |---|---|---|
   | enim, nam | for | "now", "indeed" |
   | ergo, igitur, ideo | therefore, so, then | "now", "and", "indeed" |
   | sed / autem, marking the **minor premiss or next step** | now | (omitted) |
   | sed / autem, adversative | but | (omitted) |
   | autem, continuative | and | (omitted), and **not "now"** |
   | cum (concessive / causal) | although / since | "whenever" |
   | tamen | yet, nevertheless | "therefore" |
   | item | again, also | "now" |
   | vero (continuative) | and; but (adversative) | "now", "truly" |
   | verumtamen | nevertheless | "but truly" |
   | unde | hence, so | "whence" (archaic), "where" |
   | per idem | by the same token | "in the same way" |
   | ymmo | indeed, rather (correcting upward) | (omitted) |
   | patet; constat | it is clear; it is evident | |

   *nunc … aliquando* ("at one time … at another") is never "now". "Now" is reserved for the minor premiss, or the step that brings a new premiss to bear (*Illos
   autem parentes debemus …*, 1.17). An *autem* that only extends the same step is "and" (*Illis autem
   cultis …*, 1.19). ⚠ The commonest error in *De ecclesia* (close-out, ~50 of 69 connective fixes): a
   *sed* that brings the minor premiss of a syllogism closed by *ergo / ideo / igitur* rendered "but".
   Before writing "but" for a *sed*, look ahead for the *ergo*: if one follows, it is "now".
3. **Ordinary words with a medieval sense:** GLOSSARY §D; look up any word whose classical and
   Wyclifite senses may differ, and propose additions.
4. **Syntax:** construe before rendering (main verb, subject, what every relative and genitive
   hangs on). Keep quantifier order where it is meaning (*nullus papa … caput*; *quilibet papa*).
   Keep Wyclif's long periods where English tolerates them. **A doubtful construal is never chosen
   silently:** translate the likelier reading and write a **C** note giving the alternative.

## Glossary (the 284 rows these chunks use; the full file is GLOSSARY.md)

# GLOSSARY — FROZEN 2026-09-28 (Wilson's pilot review)

*One Latin term, one English rendering, every time, across the whole corpus. A reader must be able
to recognise the same word coming back, because Wyclif's arguments turn on it. A rendering changes
only by a dated decision in CONVENTIONS' decision log, never silently in a chunk. Entries may be
*added* by recorded decision as new terms appear. **Tag keys:** a chunk tags a term as
`[English]{g:latin-form}` (CONVENTIONS §11), and the key must match a Latin form in one of the rows
below. These rows are also the source of the site's hover glossary. Where a term has two senses,
the entry says so and each chunk's note picks one. A term marked **CONTEXTUAL** holds together
senses English splits: its English is chosen by context, under the dikaiosune rule (CONVENTIONS §6).*

**Sources of the decisions.** "Pilot" = settled in `pilots/` this session. "T. p. N" = Mark
Thakkar, "Duces caecorum," *Vivarium* 58 (2020), page N (`reference/`), which documents the
errors of the two most recent translations. His corrections are adopted here unless an entry says
otherwise.

**Orthography.** Headwords are given in the Society editions' medieval spelling (*eciam*,
*racio*, *quiditas*, *ymmo*), because that is what the translator will meet on the page.

## ¶2 · 398.3–398.27

> [p. 398] Capitulum primum premittens equivocacionem quadruplicem potestatis dividit ipsam, declarans quomodo ex scriptura non docetur, quod est potestas preter racionalis nature potenciam, et quomodo aliquid alterius subiacet potestati. Secundo descendendo ad divisionem Hugonis elicit descripcionem potestatis secularis et spiritualis et obiciendo dissolvit. Tercio principaliter arguitur quod superfluit ponere aliam potestatem preter liberum arbitrium, secundo quod potestas ordinis, cum nunc potest laxari et nunc restringi sufficit sine potestate regiminis vel iurisdicionis superaddita. Et tercio arguitur per hoc, quod creatura non cooperatur cum Deo comproducendo quantitatem indivisibilem in mente, quo tripliciter confirmat et respondet, quod potestas nunc accipitur simpliciter et nunc aggregative superaddita humana promulgacione autentica, et illam necesse est ponere post lapsum, quando Deus autenticat, et sic sunt multe potestates, que non fuissent in statu innocencie; secundo docet quomodo potest augeri vel minui, et tercio docet quid creatura coefficit cum Deo et quid non, et exponendo scripturas multiplices notat qualiter Christus tacuit, Joh. XIX tacendo doctrinas multiplices.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶3 · 399.1–399.18

> [p. 399] Capitulum secundum obicit tripliciter et dissolvit, primo suadet tripliciter, quod nemo habet potestatem ad faciendum aliud nisi quod Deus intendit quod faciat, quod concedit declarans sensum, et quomodo potestas talis perditur ad non gradum. Secundo obiciens de potestate faciendi miracula ostendit quid est miraculum, et non potest creature ut talis competere, docet quomodo est de miraculis senciendum, secundo principaliter docet quomodo senciendum est de potestate spiritus sancti et gracie, et tercio diffuse declarat quomodo sacerdos non tollit peccata, sed dum fuerit conformis divino iudicio quodammodo remittit et absolvit hominem a peccato. Quarto declarat potestatem excomunicandi et puniendi subiectos, et quinto tractat de modo committendi potestates, de origine et perdicione earum et que sit alia maior.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶4 · 399.19–399.30

> [p. 399] Capitulum tercium probat via triplici, quod P. habuit primatum inter apostolos primo octuplici testimonio evangelii, et exponit, quomodo sathan expetivit ut cribraret apostolos. Secundo probat illud octuplici testimonio factorum Petri, et tercio octuplici testimonio sanctorum. Quarto vero narrando triplicem fructum huius sentencie declarat quod Petrus propter humilitatem in moribus constitutus est a Christo caput ecclesie, et declarando quomodo Petrus precellebat fide, humilitate et caritate, declarat quomodo Petrus Johanne magis dilexerat.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶5 · 399.31–401.27

> [p. 399] Capitulum quartum declarat primo quod Petrus non solum accepit illum primatum pro se ipso, sed pro eius vicariis succedentibus in ecclesia militante. Secundo declarat quod, sicut Petrus exhinc fuit per se Christi vicarius, quod suum [p. 400] magistrum quoad actus et mores secutus fuit conformius, sic oportet P. vicarium similius sequi ipsum. Tercio docet, quod humana eleccio non valet, nisi de quanto promulgaverit conformiter ad divinam. Quarto quod precise de tanto debet subditus sequi suum privatum prepositum, de quanto ipse sequitur Ihesum nostrum. Quinto sive electores bene vel male elegerint operibus meritoriis est credendum, et quicumque modus noster eligendi prepositum fuerit eleccioni Dei conformior est eo melior, et obicit quadrupliciter et dissolvit, declarans quod de quatuor modis eligendi prepositum eleccio persortem foretmodo securior. Sexto notat, quod non docetur ex scriptura, quomodo preeminencia Petri super alios apostolos stat in universali iurisdicione super totam militantem ecclesiam, aut quod particularis potestas iurisdicionis debet esse in alios apostolos per illam potestatem ad locum et populum limitata. Capitulum quintum declarat quintupliciter sextam sentenciam, quod Romanus pontifex debet racione officii vicarii sequi Christum in altissima paupertate, et post obicit tripliciter et dissolvit. Primo per hoc quod papa non posset perficere suum officium nec honorifice tenere statum papalem sine tali dominio, et declarat oppositum per Bernhardum. Secundo obicit per hoc, quod multi sancti episcopi approbarunt dotacionem huiusmodi et dissolvit, declarans quantum malum contingit ex isto porismate, cum sequimur mixtim bona secundum racionem qua sunt mala. Tercio obicit per hoc, quod est distinccio inter potestatem iurisdicionis et ordinis, eo quod solus papa habet potestatem super universalem ecclesiam; non enim est aliud proprium pape officium, et dissolvit, concedens distinccionem et negans [p. 401] illam universalem potestatem et obiciendo tripliciter ex domino Ardmacano dissolvit, declarans quod Paulus non habuit a Petro sed a Christo licenciam predicandi, et concludit quod, sicut apostoli erant socii et amici, sic debet esse hodie, cum ditacio non dissolveret amiciciam, eo quod Christus non inveniret amicos inter nostros episcopos conversando et declarat in tribus conclusionibus pastoris officium. Capitulum sextum connectit cum declaracione septimam sentenciam et octavam, quarum prior dicit sic: Quod sicut nullus apostolorum habuit tantam potestatem quantam habuit Christus humanitus, sic nec omnis papa habuit tantam potestatem quantam habuit beatus Petrus vel alius Christi apostolus, et declarando utrumque membrum tripliciter obicit et dissolvit. Octava sentencia dicit quod Romano pontifice vel quocumque alio fingente se gerere primatum vicarie potestatis Christi in terris ab eius semita declinante secundum sensum scripture nullus plenius vel periculosius gerit nomen et opus Antichristi; quod probat ex condicionibus undecim, annectens materiam de quiditate et divisione status, quomodo componitur ex non quantis et, declarans quomodo status potest alterari, tangit quomodo subditus potest prelatum corripere et dissolvit.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶6 · 401.28–402.33

> [p. 401] Capitulum septimum discurrit per illa octo argumenta, quibus probatur ex scriptura quod Petrus debet habere primatum super alios apostolos et per consequens papa super alios episcopos; primo igitur declarat quod primatus iste non infert dominacionem vel preeminenciam secularem, quod probat ex triplici testimonio Bernhardi; Augustini et Crysostomi. Secundo declarat [p. 402] quod papa debet esse capitaneus, precedens in spirituali exercitu pacificando, et declarat quod est clavis ad sensum scripture et sanctorum doctorum. Tercio ostendit quomodo papa debet ambulare super aquas ad Dominum instar Petri, et exponit propheciam Apocalipsis XII de dracone stante ante mulierem parituram. Quarto exponit ad quadruplicem sensum quomodo Christi vicarius debet cum Petro se mittere in mare. Quinto declarat quomodo papa debet vigilare cum Christo, et quod aliter est secundum Crysostomum Antichristus, et quomodo illud Matth. XXIV: Multi venient in nomine meo, dicentes quia ego sum Christus, verificatur de falsis Christi vicariis et quomodo regna istis papis concertantibus racionabiliter responderent, et quomodo pastores ex dicto beati Gregorii periculosius maculant gregem Dei. Sexto declarat prelati periculum ex dictis Lyncolniensis et quod foret remedium, et quomodo CLIII magni pisces signant totam ecclesiam Christi, et quomodo papa niteretur facere similiter. Septimo declaratur superioritas pape per hoc, quod in prima synodo, de qua Act.

## For the coordinator

1. **I11b-4.** The old note says Harris leaves the place blank. That is wrong: Harris's Index of references (latinworks03 l. 22455–22486) gives *De Civ. Dei x. 29*. The replacement records both his place and the true verbal source.
2. **I11b-6.** The old note credited Augustine with "the Holy Spirit is not the Son". He says the opposite way round: Christ is not the Spirit's son. Check whether the English at 193.20–21 or the gloss implies otherwise.
3. **I11a-1.** Wyclif presents 3° and 4° as Scotus's own arguments. In the *Ordinatio* they are reasons Scotus reports from others and rejects (art. 3, n. 7), so Wyclif may be using a *reportatio*, or misreading. The *Doctor arguit* in the English stands as Wyclif's claim.
4. **I10g-1.** No manuscript reads 9. O's omission of *3°* (183.11) does not bear on the distinction.
5. **Harris's index** maps Wyclif's *Ench.* "29" here to c. 36 (11.36), while RESULTS-13 maps the same "29" at p. 167 to 12.38. Wyclif's chapter numbers are not consistent, so judge each case by its content.

## Latin doubts (for the plates)

- **18.31** (01-e) *ne effectualis efficacia operis sequeretur*: the argument needs "that … would *not* follow". Check the apparatus and plate n0079 for a *non* lost before *sequeretur* (or *ne non*). If the plate has none, this is our emendation (row 3).
- **101.3** (05-f) *veniens in mundum illum*: the plate (R3) confirms *illum*, but the English drops it. The sense wants *ille* (he, Christ, coming into the world). Otherwise it is "that world", which is odd. Translate it, or give a T note that gives the print and the suspicion.
- **side-note 16.22** (01-e) "the acts orising from it": *orising* is presumably "arising". This is an OCR scar or Loserth's misprint, to check on n0077.
- **side-note 18.23** (01-e) "Righteousness in the chief sign of power": "in" is presumably "is the". Check on n0079.
- **31.13** (02-b) *quasi contritus*: n. 18 suspects a scar. This review takes the print as sound (medieval *quasi* = "as", row 12). No plate is needed unless the coordinator disagrees.

**01-e:** sound and close to the Latin. Two sentences need repair: 19.6–7 does not construe, and the *ne* at 18.31 has been emended silently under a C note. One construal across Loserth's stop (17.25) should be disclosed.

**02-b:** a long, well-handled period. All the findings are local: a few glossary renderings drift from their tags (*ignorare*, *dicitur*), the *color* C note is missing, one agent is supplied without a note ("[the gloss]"), and *quasi contritus* is read as "as it were" against the medieval sense.

**03-c:** clean. The fixes are a scholastic *probabiliter*, *pro se* (Christ, not Peter), and three missing first-occurrence tags.

**05-f:** good overall, with one real error: *laxacio* rendered "relaxing", and a note that inverts the bishop's widening of his own power. Several construals were chosen silently (*sibi habiles*, *quod si*, *graciose ecclesie*), and *infideliter* comes out as "faithlessly".

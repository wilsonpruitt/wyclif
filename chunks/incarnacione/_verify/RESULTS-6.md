## For the coordinator to decide

1. **I05a-4 and I05c-1: emend the numbers?** In both rows the source supports O against the print: Thomas q. *50*, not *70*, and Augustine tract. *48*, not *44*. The T notes only record O's reading. The coordinator should decide whether to print O's number in the text, or keep the print and leave the case to the note. Harris himself identifies q. 50 and tract. 48.9 in his note and index.
2. **The "Ome." offset.** Here "Ome. 47" is the received number. "Ome. 44" for 48 is an error of four, not the 50/51 offset of one, so it reads as a scribal slip (xliv/xlviii) that O does not share.
3. **Chrysostom (I05a-1)** was checked in translation only. A Latin text (medieval version, or the Latin side of PG 52) would allow wording to be compared, but Wyclif quotes no words, so sense is enough.
4. **I05c-2:** the fragment fits *nec aliam substancialem essenciam* and the time/motion examples. *Ne fiat vacuum vel defectus in Deo* was not found there. It may be in parts of tr. 6 that Dziewicki did not print.
5. Harris's p. 78 entry for *De Adnichilacione* is a second citation, perhaps in a later chunk. Reuse this identification there.

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **07-d, p. 117**: ***Sed** quia Verbum ex integro est illa humanitas … patet quod …*, rendered "Now since". The opinion just reported makes the Word's suffering conditional on his being personally the humanity; the *sed* supplies that condition and the conclusion follows (*patet*, then *Firmiter itaque*). (a) "Now" fits; (b) "But", against the reported opinion's reluctance, is the other reading.
- **07-d, p. 118**: ***Sed** pro nomine 'habitus' est notandum*, rendered "But". It opens a new head (the senses of *habitus*), which is a transition, not a contrast. "Now" (next step) would also do; "But" as a transition is defensible.
- **08-a, p. 126**: ***Sed** quia Christus est gigas gemine substancie … ideo dicitur humanitas … accidens*, rendered "But because … therefore". This is (b): the humanity is not an accident *simpliciter*, yet for this reason it is called one. The *ideo* closes a *quia … ideo*, not a syllogism, so "now" is not called for.
- **08-b, p. 127** (Augustine): *Quid **autem** propter quid habeat, dinoscendum est*, rendered "But". Either (b), distinguishing what belongs by which nature, set against the unity of person just stated, or (c) "And", the next point. "But" is defensible.
- **08-b, p. 128** (Grosseteste): *Quid **autem** sit homo … diffiniamus*, rendered "But". It moves from the exposition of Gen 1:26 to the definition, which is a next step ("Now") or a continuation ("And") more than a contrast. It is a toss-up inside a quotation, so it is left alone.
- **08-d, p. 135**: ***Sed** voluntas divina non potest ex istis causare creatam personam*, rendered "Now". It is the premiss that, joined to "no creature causes except as subject to the divine will", shows the minor false. No *ergo* is written, but the conclusion is the *Hic dicitur quod minor est falsa* it supports. "Now" is defensible; "But" would also read.
- **07-d, p. 119** (not a connective error): *accidit Deo humanitas, **sed** non⁹ inseparabiliter* is rendered "but inseparably", with no "not". Figure 9 is on *non*, and note [^10] presumably reports a manuscript that omits it. The *sed* is "but" on either reading. Confirm that [^10] is a T note for the reading adopted.
- Not counted: three unbold *sed* carrying footnote figures (08-a p. 124 *Aliud enim, sed⁷ non alius*; 08-b p. 130 *Sed³ procul hoc*; 08-d p. 136 *sed⁵ Christus conceptus est*). They are rendered "but", "But" and "now" respectively, all correct for their class.

# Connective check: *sed* / *autem*, De potestate pape, wave 3 (chunks 04-a – 05-d)

Classes: (a) minor premiss / next step, "now" · (b) adversative, "but" · (c) continuative *autem*, "and";
*non … sed* (incl. *non solum / nedum … sed*, *ne … sed*), "but".

## Doubtful, left as they are

- **04-a, p. 62**: *Sed antequam discuciatur utrum oporteat ipsum esse Romanum pontificem necessarium*, rendered "But before". A turn in the exposition: (b), deferring the Roman question, or (a), the next step ("Now before"). "But" defensible.
- **04-e, p. 76**: *Stultum autem et infidele mendacium foret fingere*, rendered "And it would be". (c), drawing the point from the Catholicon list, or (b), against the supposition that the apostles depended on Peter. "And" defensible.
- **05-c, p. 89**: *Sed tercia stulticia est qua arguimus*, rendered "But the third folly". The *sed* moves along a list (*Secunda stulticia est … Sed tercia*); it is not really adversative, but "now" would be odd here and the card gives no "and" for *sed*. Left as "But".
- **05-a, p. 81**: *decretiste et fratres, sed specialiter fratres Minores*, rendered "but especially". Intensive *sed specialiter* (cf. wave 1 *sed et*); "but especially" carries it.

# *De eucharistia*, De eucharistia et penitencia, chapter 1: the two sacraments in which the church is deluded, and the newness of confession

## Headnote

*De eucharistia et penitencia sive de confessione* is a short work in six chapters, printed by Loserth after the *Tractatus de eucharistia* (pp. 327–348). Only the first chapter is on the eucharist: it names the eucharist and penance as the two sacraments in which Antichrist has deluded the church, restates the case of the long treatise in a page (the host is naturally bread and sacramentally the body of Christ), and turns to penance. The remaining chapters argue that confession to a priest, as the canon *Omnis utriusque sexus* requires it, is a recent papal institution, not of the necessity of salvation. The manuscripts' titles vary (*De confessione*, *De eucharistia et penitencia*, *De sacramento*; see the Transcription notes). Loserth (Introduction §6) gives no date: he describes it as the development of Wyclif's doctrine of penance in general and of confession in particular, already known from his lesser tracts and sermons. The work is commonly placed among Wyclif's last writings, c. 1382–84 [verify].

**Sigla** (Introd. §6; they differ from those of the *Tractatus de eucharistia*): **A** Vienna 3927; **B** Bautzen, Gersdorf Library M. St. 80.7; **C** Vienna 1337; **D** Prague Univ. III G 11; **E** Vienna 3980; **F** Vienna 4527. Scripture chapters are Wyclif's, verse numbers Loserth's.

## ¶1 · 329.2–330.18

> [p. 329] Duo sunt sacramenta precipua in quibus ecclesia est illusa, scilicet sacramentum eukaristie et sacramentum penitencie. In sacramento eukaristie Antichristus paulative tantum seduxit ecclesiam quod fecit eam quiditatem huius sacramenti sensibilis ignorantem. Superaddit eciam quod ignorat quod quilibet sacerdos quantumcunque indignus fuerit conficiendo facit regulariter ultra hoc quod fecerunt patres veteris testamenti vel apostoli miracula infinita. Sed miraculum istud est imperceptibile atque inutile. Imperceptibile quidem, quia post consecracionem sensus ut prius renunciant quod est panis; et inutile est, quia per confeccionem suam (ut false inquiunt) panis destruitur, ymmo materia prima que est fundamentum nature secundum se totam desinit esse, ac si Deus in mundi principio materiam crearet superfluam vel aliter pro sacerdotibus Antichristi discipulis materiam primam creavit in mundi principio, ut ex ea blaspheme et irregulariter consecrent corpus Christi et postmodum sine causa miraculose ipsam adnichilent. Talia autem ridicula infinita que fideles evacuant, dicendo quod hoc [p. 330] sacramentum naturaliter est panis et sacramentaliter corpus Christi. Non enim vident causam quare Deus hoc fundamentum nature destrueret, sicut nec vident quod corpus Christi in natura sua est materialius, densius aut maius quam prius fuerat; nec vident in quid ista materia convertatur nisi secundum fantasiam suam erroneam in nichilum convertatur, quia utendo terminis de conversione (ut prius usa fuit ecclesia) illa materia non converteretur in materiam corporis Christi vel in corpus Christi aut eius partem nisi fieret id conversum, sicut nos dicimus quod panis convertitur in corpus Christi et prius iniustus in filium Dei est conversus; quod omnis talis conversio dicit aliquam faccionem, sic quod natura conversi sit accidentaliter de novo quod vel cuiusmodi prius non fuerat. Materiam autem istam non possunt fingere sic converti, ideo necessitantur dicere quod virtute sui miraculi est annichilata. Sed procul a fidelibus talis vesania.

There are two chief [sacraments]{g:sacramentum} in which the [church]{g:ecclesia} has been deluded: namely the sacrament of [the Eucharist]{g:eukaristia} and the sacrament of penance. In the sacrament of the Eucharist Antichrist has little by little so far seduced the church that he has made it [ignorant of]{g:ignorare} the [quiddity]{g:quiditas} of this [perceptible]{g:sensibilis} sacrament.[^1] He adds besides that it is ignorant that any [priest]{g:sacerdos}, however unworthy he may be, in [consecrating]{g:conficere} does, [as a rule]{g:regulariter}, beyond what the fathers of the old testament or the apostles did, numberless miracles.[^2] But this miracle is imperceptible and useless. Imperceptible, for after the [consecration]{g:consecracio} the senses report as before "it is bread"; and useless it is, for by their [consecration]{g:confeccio} (as they falsely say) the [bread]{g:panis} is destroyed; indeed [prime matter]{g:materia-prima}, which is the foundation of [nature]{g:natura}, ceases to be in its whole self; as if God at the beginning of the world created matter superfluous, or else created prime matter at the beginning of the world for the priests, the disciples of Antichrist, so that out of it they might blasphemously and [irregularly]{g:irregulariter} consecrate [the body of Christ]{g:corpus-christi} and afterwards, without cause, miraculously [annihilate]{g:adnichilare} it.[^3] And such are the numberless absurdities that the faithful do away with, by saying "this [330] sacrament is naturally bread and [sacramentally]{g:sacramentaliter} the body of Christ". For they do not see a cause why God should destroy this foundation of nature, just as they do not see that the body of Christ is in its nature more material, denser or greater than it had been before; nor do they see into what this matter is converted, unless, according to their erroneous [imagination]{g:fantasia}, it is converted into nothing;[^4] for, using the terms about [conversion]{g:conversio} (as the church formerly used them), that matter would not be converted into the matter of the body of Christ, or into the body of Christ or a part of it, unless it became that into which it was converted, as we say "the bread is converted into the body of Christ" and "one formerly unjust has been converted into a son of God"; for every such conversion [signifies]{g:dicere} some [making]{g:faccio}, so that the nature of the thing converted is, accidentally, anew what, or of what sort, it had not been before.[^5] Now they cannot [feign]{g:fingere} this matter to be so converted; therefore they are compelled to say "by virtue of their miracle it has been annihilated". But far from the faithful be such madness.

## ¶2 · 330.19–331.22

> [p. 330] In inpossibiles autem errores incidunt Antichristi filii de penitencia, cum nesciunt fundare istam penitenciam quam papa de novo instituit; evidenciam autem que fidem faceret vel scripture nescit; nescit eciam si penitens sit contritus vel peccatum suum deletum a Domino, sicut nescit quantitatem vel qualitatem penitencie iniungende. Communiter tamen limitatur quiditas huius penitencie quod est peccati ostensio sacerdoti cum contricione precedente et satisfaccione penitencie consequente; et sic (ut quidam inquiunt) sacramentum penitencie est in diversis generibus; nec est possibile tres eius partes, scilicet cordis contricionem, oris confessionem et operis satisfaccionem temporaliter [p. 331] esse simul. Spreta ista irregulari descripcione penitencie videtur in ipsam ulterius procedendum, et primo notanda est sentencia sepedicta quod non est simpliciter de necessitate salutis quod quilibet beatificandus eciam dum discrecionem attigerit confiteatur omnia peccata sua que commisserat proprio sacerdoti, quia antequam ista lex fuit edita multi sancti fuerant salvati tam de lege antiqua quam de lege gracie. Et sic videtur Spiritum Sanctum negligenter sacramentum tam necessarium obmisisse. Nam Actuum IIᵒ et infra legitur Petrum, Paulum et ceteros apostolos multa milia populi convertisse. Et non recolo aliquem conversum istud novum sacramentum a sacerdote aliquo accepisse, nec Christus nec Baptista nec aliquis apostolus ista sacramentali penitencia quam nos cognoscimus usus fuit. Ideo dicit doctor decretorum Johannes de Deo quod istud sacramentum a quadam institucione pape cepit originem, quem quidam dicunt fuisse Innocencium IIIᵘᵐ; et confirmacio patet De Penitenciis et Remissionibus capitulo Omnis utriusque sexus. Et ista confessio est alibi multipliciter impugnata.

And the sons of Antichrist fall into impossible errors about penance, since they do not know how to ground this penance which the [pope]{g:papa} has newly instituted;[^6] and he does not know evidence that would make faith, or [evidence] of scripture; he does not know either whether the penitent is contrite or his sin blotted out by the Lord, just as he does not know the [quantity]{g:quantitas} or [quality]{g:qualitas} of the penance to be enjoined.[^7] Yet the quiddity of this penance is commonly [assigned]{g:limitare} thus: that it is the showing of sin to a priest, with contrition going before and the [satisfaction]{g:satisfaccio} of penance following; and so (as some say) the sacrament of penance is in diverse genera;[^8] nor is it possible for its three parts, namely contrition of heart, confession of mouth and satisfaction of work, to be together in time. [331] With this irregular [definition]{g:descripcio} of penance spurned, it seems we must proceed further into it; and first is to be noted the [teaching]{g:sentencia} often stated:[^9] that it is not [without qualification]{g:simpliciter} of the necessity of salvation that everyone who is to be beatified, even once he has reached discretion, confess all the sins he had committed to his own priest; for before this law was published many [saints]{g:sancti} had been saved, both of the old law and of [the law of grace]{g:lex-gracie}. And so it would seem that the Holy Spirit negligently left out so necessary a sacrament. For in Acts 2 and below one reads that Peter, Paul and the other apostles converted many thousands of the people.[^10] And I do not recall that anyone converted received this new sacrament from any priest; nor did Christ, nor the Baptist, nor any apostle use this sacramental penance that we know. Therefore the doctor of decrees John of God says that this sacrament took its origin from a certain institution of a pope, whom some say was Innocent III;[^11] and the confirmation is clear in [the title] On Penances and Remissions, the chapter *Omnis utriusque sexus*.[^12] And this confession has been attacked elsewhere in many ways.

## Notes

[^1]: **G.** *Quiditas*, quiddity: what a thing is. The new work's first occurrence; the long treatise argued that the quiddity of the host is bread.

[^2]: **C/T.** *Superaddit eciam quod ignorat quod …*: read with Antichrist as subject and the church as subject of *ignorat*: the church does not see that its doctrine makes every priest a worker of numberless miracles. Loserth's side-note takes the clause as Antichrist's teaching ("He would have us believe …"), as if *quod ignorat* were "what it does not know". D reads *ignoraret*. *Patres* is Loserth's emendation; the manuscripts read *plures*, "more [men]".

[^3]: **C/T.** *Confeccio* and *consecracio* both stand here; both are "consecration", since they are not set against each other. *Suam* in *per confeccionem suam* is the priests' (their consecration). C reads *discipulus … creaverit*; 329.19 records *irrevocabiliter* (for *irregulariter*?), siglum lost in the OCR.

[^4]: **C.** *Non enim vident*: the faithful see no cause; *fantasiam suam erroneam* turns to the adversaries, whose imagination it is.

[^5]: **C.** *Nisi fieret id conversum*: matter is converted into something only if it becomes the thing into which it is converted (BC *illud conversum*). Hence the adversaries, who deny that prime matter becomes the body, must say it is annihilated. *Dicit*: "signifies", the logical use.

[^6]: **T.** *In* is Loserth's addition (*Codd.: in deest. Addidi*); without it, *inpossibiles … errores incidunt* would be "fall into" with a bare accusative.

[^7]: **T/C.** The manuscripts read *Evidencia autem que fidem faceret vel scripture nescit eciam si …*; Loserth prints *evidenciam* and a second *nescit*. AB read *nescit* for *nesciunt* in the clause before. The singular *nescit* takes the pope as subject; it could be any such confessor.

[^8]: **C.** *Limitatur*: "is assigned", i.e. fixed by a definition. *In diversis generibus*: its parts fall in different categories, an act of the will, a spoken act, a work, so it is not one thing.

[^9]: **C/T.** *Sentencia*, "teaching": Wyclif's own position, stated before. D reads *sentencia secundo differencia*; ACV read *Supposita ista* for *Spreta ista* (331.1), C *regulariri* (!).

[^10]: **S.** Acts 2:41 (three thousand) and 4:4 (five thousand); Wyclif names Paul as well.

[^11]: **S/R.** *Doctor decretorum Johannes de Deo*: John of God, canonist; the work and place are to be checked (LEDGER Kpen01-1), and so is the attribution to Innocent III (LEDGER Kpen01-2). D's margin: *Confessionem auricularem instituit Innocencius III*; B's margin: *Fundata quando et a quo fuit penitencia communiter dicta*.

[^12]: **S.** *Omnis utriusque sexus*: X 5.38.12 (Loserth: *Decret. Greg.* lib. V, tit. XXXVIII, cap. XII; the modern form is ours), the Fourth Lateran Council's canon 21 (LEDGER Kpen01-3), which binds everyone *postquam ad annos discretionis pervenerit* to confess all sins *proprio sacerdoti* once a year, the words Wyclif echoes above.

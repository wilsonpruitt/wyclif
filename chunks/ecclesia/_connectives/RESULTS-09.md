## Doubtful, left as they are

- **22-g, p. 539**: *Sed et idem videtur per se notorium considerando …*, rendered "But the same also seems evident of itself". *Sed et* adds a second proof of the maxim; (b) as rendered, or (a)/(c) the next step ("And the same also").
- **22-g, p. 539**: *Item, videtur quod murmur tale sit contra dominum quem debemus credere omnem hominem velle mori, sed voluntati Dei non est licitum taliter contrariare*, rendered "; but it is not lawful so to oppose the will of God". (b), or (a), the second premiss of an argument whose conclusion (such murmuring is unlawful) is left unstated; no *ergo*, so left by the 08 precedent (22-a, p. 519).
- **22-g, p. 541**: Augustine (Decretum), *Quamvis cure pro mortuis non … prosint, sed hiis tantum …; sed quia non discernimus qui sint illi, oportet …*, second *sed* rendered "yet because". Adversative carried by "yet" as the correlate of "Although"; could be "but because". Accepted (see Totals).
- **22-g, p. 542**: *Cavere autem debemus de malicia*, rendered "And we must beware". (c), or (b), a caution set against the preceding "now well, now badly".
- **23-b, p. 553**: Aquinas, *Racio autem quare valere possunt est unitas corporis mistici*, rendered "Now the reason why they can avail". (a), the reason brought to bear on the thesis just stated, or (c) "And the reason".
- **23-b, p. 553**: Aquinas, *cum unus pro alio satisfacere potest, sancti autem in quibus superhabundancia … invenitur, non determinate pro isto … fecerunt*, rendered ", and the saints". In substance (a), the minor of the argument whose conclusion is *papa distribuit ea dispariter*; but it stands inside a *cum* clause, where "now" will not go into English, so "and" is kept.
- **23-f, p. 565**: *Minor autem sic ostenditur*, rendered "Now the minor is shown thus". (a), the step to the minor, or (c) "And the minor", following *Assumptum patet*.
- **23-g, p. 569**: *Ideo restant secunda via et tercia concedende; sed constat non oportere sumptuose acquirere tales bullas*, rendered "; but it is evident". (b), or (a), the next step drawn from the two ways granted.
- **22-f, p. 536**: *Sed quia dixit sacrum Psalmum intelligere de membris suis predestinatis …*, rendered "But because". (b), or (c), moving on within the Augustine paraphrase.
- **22-f, p. 537**: Enchiridion 110, *pro valde malis sunt vivencium consolaciones, sed mediocriter malis faciunt dampnaciones tollerabiliores*, rendered "; but for the moderately bad". Fourth member of a list; (b) as rendered (no help vs. some help), or (c).

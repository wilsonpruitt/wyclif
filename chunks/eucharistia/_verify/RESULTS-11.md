## For the coordinator

1. **K05b-3 is a wrong locus.** The passage is *Enchiridion* 53, not 52. Wyclif's *LII* is his own slip: he gives *LIII* when he quotes it later.
2. **K05b-2.** Only Thomas uses the word *haeresis*. Bonaventure has *error pessimus*. Decide whether the English note should keep "naming this opinion a heresy" for both of them.
3. **K05c-5 stays editor.** The rule is attested only through Loserth's quotation of the gloss on c. 42 (34.22). The glossed Decretum itself is still unread.
4. **K05d-2.** c. 36 is only a "cf." The note no longer presents it as the source.
5. **K05d-5.** The Lombard source may bear on K05d-4: Wyclif's "Anselm" summary may come from the Lombard's d. 11 c. 6 rather than from *Ep.* IV.107.

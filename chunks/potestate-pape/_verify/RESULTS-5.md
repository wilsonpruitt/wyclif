## For the coordinator

1. **P06i-2.** Loserth's reference is wrong in a way that can be explained. X 5.32.2 shares the incipit *Quum ex iniuncto*. The correct reference is X 5.7.12.
2. **P06h-4.** Caiaphas is not in q. 35. He is in q. 101, so Wyclif has combined two *Quaestiones*. Also, q. 35's Nebuchadnezzar is the furnace vision (Dan. 3:92), not the dream of Dan. 2. Please decide whether the note should say Wyclif conflates.
3. **P06h-1.** In Higden the voice is the *hostis antiquus* (the devil), while Wyclif writes *angelica vox*. Please decide whether the note should flag this, as the proposed change does.
4. **Two of Loserth's columns are off.** Boethius is PL 63, 724, not 720 (P06h-6). Bernard *De consid.* III.1.2 is PL 182, 759, not 769 (P07a-7).
5. **P06g-1.** The note's guess of *De ecclesia* is wrong. The list is the canonists' consent verse. Wyclif counts it as six in *De simonia* and *De off. regis* p. 68, but as five here and at *De off. regis* p. 117.
6. **Still open.** P06g-2 (Ps.-Chrysostom hom. 26, Loserth's "(?)") and P07a-1 (the Ambrose "epistola") remain. So do the young-virgins clause of P06g-5 and the old-number equation *De verbis Domini* s. 8 = *Sermo* 67 (P07a-2).

## For the coordinator

1. **P08d-1: a wrong chapter.** The *Polychronicon* passage (V, 140) is book IV, ch. **26**. The codices' XIV and Loserth's emended XXXVI are both wrong. The English (08-d ¶, "book 4, chapter 36") translates the print. Should the text keep the print's chapter, or should it be emended to XXVI?
2. **P08g-8: Loserth's references are wrong.** The passage is Sermo 129.9 and not Sermo 130 or 46. The fix is above.
3. **P08g-2.** The note's conjecture *quod Deus avertat* is false. Should the English at *quod Deus magis facit* be revisited in the light of Augustine's *hoc magis ipse scit*? Augustine also has *in nomine suo* where Wyclif has *in nomine Christi* (08-g n6), which is worth a T line there.
4. **P08g-3.** "Superius" points to ch. 7 (148–149), not ch. 8.
5. **P08d-5.** Wyclif's *venio Romam* agrees with Ambrose (*Venio*), not FitzRalph (*Vado*). Perhaps add an R note.
6. **P08c-1.** 08-c has no notes yet. When the notes are written, the S note should cite VII.18, fol. 50r col. b, and say that the "dispares" wording is the interlocutor's (VII.17).
7. **P08e-6.** The *Recte* years and Wyclif's clauses on Guido of Crema and John's reconciliation go beyond p. 303. The John of Struma entry is in Diceto vol. II (1178), not seen.
8. **P08f-5** stays untraced.

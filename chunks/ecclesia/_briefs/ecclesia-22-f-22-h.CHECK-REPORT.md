## SOURCE ITEMS
A. E22f-3: Aug. De civ. 21.24 (PL 41) reads "Quod quidem non ideo confirmo, quoniam non resisto" (Wyclif: *Hoc quidem*). E22f-4: the passage is in 21.17: "Extendatur ergo ac profundatur fons hujus misericordiae ... Verum si aliquis audet, vincit nempe istos, et tamen tanto invenitur errare deformius, et contra recta Dei verba perversius, quanto sibi videtur sentire clementius." Wyclif's "Si quis, inquit, audeat ista asserere" is his own reworking, and *videtur sentire sibi*. Not a verbatim quotation: the reworded part is wrapped in Loserth's italics as if verbatim.
B. E22f-8: Ench. 110 (PL 40, TEI 7406 "De fide spe et charitate") has three classes (valde bonis / non valde malis / valde malis "etiamsi nulla sunt adiumenta ... vivorum consolationes"). The fourth is only the closing clause: "Quibus autem prosunt ... aut certe ut tolerabilior fiat ipsa damnatio" (same as Friedberg c.23). Ench. 93 also has *tolerabiliorem ... damnationem*. Wyclif's "mediocriter malis" class is his construction. No separate fourth class.
C. E22g-1/2/9/10/11, E22h-2: De cura 18.22 (PL 40) = Friedberg c.19, and the print's footnote gives cap. XVIII. It contains "Non existimemus ..." (Decr. *estimemus*), "quamvis non pro quibus fiunt omnibus prosint" (Wyclif's "cure pro mortuis ... pro omnibus" is his own wording), "Melius enim supererunt", "Diligentius tamen facit haec quisque ... quo pro illo fiant similiter a suis", "Corpori autem humando quidquid impenditur ... humanitatis officium, secundum affectum quo nemo unquam carnem suam odio habet ... ut corpori mortuo, sed tamen resurrecturo ... impensum huiusmodi officium sit etiam quodammodo eiusdem fidei testimonium". No literal "Humanitatis ... hoc est officium"; that is Wyclif's rearrangement. His "evidens" replaces *etiam*. *Curatio funeris* = De cura 2.4. *Exanimis* = De cura 3.5. All confirmed.
D. Friedberg C.13 q.2: c.16 *Quibus peccata dimissa non sunt* (inscr. Augustinus; the Correctores say it is really Gregory, Dial. 4); c.17 *Cum gravia* (Gregory, Dial. 4.50; Wyclif's "dimissa sunt et" is an addition); c.19 *Non estimemus* (Aug. De cura 18.22, with §1 *Diligentius* and §2 *Corpori*); c.22 *Animae defunctorum* (inscr. "Idem", Gregory to Boniface; the Correctores: no author found, Burchard gives Origen, Ivo Gregory). Its four ways are: oblationes sacerdotum, preces sanctorum, karorum elemosine, ieiunium cognatorum. "Stipendia annalia" is NOT in the canon (Wyclif's addition at 545.29). c.22 §1 *Curatio* (= De cura 2.4) and §2 *Nec ideo tamen* (= De cura 3.5) are inside it. c.23 *Tempus quod* (Aug. Ench. [109 and] 110). c.24 *Quia alii tertium* (Ambrose, "in lib. de obitu Theodosii"). c.25 *Quam preposterum* (Cyprian). c.26 Chrysostom, hom. 26 on Hebrews (Ubicunque sepeliamur). c.27 *Habent forsitan* (Anastasius). c.28 *Qui divina* ("ex Concilio Tolletano III [c.22]"). The inscription is confirmed; Loserth's footnote gives cap. XXVIII.
E. De mortalitate (PL 4): ch. 18 "Quam praeposterum est, quamque perversum, ut, cum ei voluntatem fieri postulemus, quando evocat nos et accersit de hoc mundo Deus, non statim voluntatis ejus imperio pareamus. Obnitimur et reluctamur ... non obsequio voluntatis" (Decr. adds "inperio ... obnitimur"). Ch. 20 "ut contestarer assidue et publice praedicarem fratres nostros non esse lugendos ... non eos amitti sed praemitti, recedentes praecedere". Wyclif follows the Decretum (c.25 §1, "Idem"). "Preceptum est, inquit" is his own inserted tag.
F. Ambrose, De obitu Theodosii §3 (PL 16, col. 1197-1198): "alii tertium diem et trigesimum, alii septimum et quadragesimum observare consueverunt, quid doceat lectio, consideremus ... Utraque ergo observatio habet auctoritatem." Wyclif follows the Decretum's rearranged order (tertium, septimum, tricesimum, quadragesimum). The "De Officiis" in Wyclif is his misattribution. The Genesis reference is Gen 50 ("sepultores Israel ... quadraginta dies"); the Deuteronomy reference is Deut 34:8.
G. 545.10-16: the "per os omnium prophetarum docuit nos temporalia relinquere" is the collect for Saturday before Pentecost in the Gregorian Sacramentary (PL 78, "Die Sabbato ante descensum fontis ... Deus, qui nobis per prophetarum ora praecepisti, temporalia relinquere, atque ad aeterna festinare"). It is a collect after the Deuteronomy canticle, not a communion; Wyclif's "post communionem" is his wording. "Terrena despicere": the postcommunion "doceas nos terrena despicere, et amare coelestia" (PL 78 and PL 74, Advent and elsewhere); it is not Pentecost-specific, matching Wyclif's "alibi".
H. E22f-2/5: NOT located. No sentence in ch. 1-22 (pp. up to 535) says that apostate or bad Christians are the worst, ascribed to Gregory. The nearest are Gregory's Moralia 34 (p. 525) and the Trajan passages. E22f-10: "elemosinis perpetuis" occurs in ch. 16 at p. 370.4 ("sic de cantariis et aliis elemosinis perpetuis"), so the ch. 16 reference stands. "Chapter 22 of book 5" was not checked.

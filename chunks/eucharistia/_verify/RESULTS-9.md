## For the coordinator

1. **K04g-1: the chapter number is wrong in the Rolls Series.** The Higden passage is VI.27 there, not VI.35, and Wyclif's text follows the CD recension. The LEDGER locus should give both numbers.
2. **K04e-1 and K04e-9 are our identifications, not Loserth's.** Wyclif names no works. Accept them, or soften them to *cf.*
3. **K04e-5: Wyclif's *De officiis* is Gratian's misinscription.** The chapter is really from *De sacramentis* VI.1.2. Decide whether the English needs to say so.
4. **K04g-5/6: the ordinary glosses need someone to read the scans.** The 1582–84 glossed Decretals and Decretum are on archive.org, but their OCR cannot be searched.

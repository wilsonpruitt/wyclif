## The volume

*Tractatus de eucharistia* and *Tractatus de penitencia sive de confessione*, ed. Johann Loserth (Wyclif
Society, 1892), from the plates of archive.org `latinworks13wycl` (**leaf = page + 71**; leaf images in
`text/eucharistia/raw/leaves/`). Three texts:

Chunk ids: `prol-a … prol-c` the compendium, `01-a … 09-n` the nine chapters, `pen01 … pen06` *De
penitencia*, `quest` the *Questio*.

- **pp. 1–10, the compendium** (*Sentencia tractatus de Eukaristia in compendio*, under "Prologus"): a
  medieval summary of the nine chapters that came down with the work. Translate it like the text.
- **pp. 11–326, the *Tractatus de eucharistia* (maior)**, nine long chapters. Written in Wyclif's last
  years, before 1382–83 (Loserth, Introd. §4: it shares passages word for word with *Sermones* III, and
  it avoids the words *substancia* and *materialis* of the bread, which Wyclif says in *Sermo* III.54 he
  has stopped using). Wyclif's case against the "modern" doctrine: that the consecrated host is in its
  nature bread and wine and sacramentally, figuratively, the body and blood of Christ; that an accident
  without a subject is impossible; that the decree *Ego Berengarius* (*De cons.* D. 2 c. 42) is the
  faith of the ancient Roman church; against the friars, Thomas, Scotus and "the sect of the signs".
  A **treatise**: Wyclif speaks in the first person, states conclusions, raises objections and answers
  them. The tractatus minor (the *Confessio*) is NOT in this volume (Shirley, *Fasc. Ziz.*).
- **pp. 327–348, *De eucharistia et penitencia sive de confessione*** in six chapters (only ch. 1 is on
  the eucharist; the rest is penance and auricular confession), then the short ***Questio ad fratres de
  sacramento altaris*** (pp. 347–348, its first part only: Loserth notes that the rest of the
  manuscript's text is not on the sacrament, and is Hus's).

**Sigla. ⚠ They differ between the two works.** *De eucharistia* (Introd. §5): **A** Vienna 3927;
**B** Vienna 3932; **C** Vienna 1387 ("the best of the three" Vienna MSS, correcting A and B); **D** Prague
Univ. IV D 22 (1404–05, Andreas of Kouřim); **E** Prague Univ. XI E 3 (1460; Loserth: "the best and most
reliable text of all the five"). Loserth's lettering "does not in any way refer to their respective
value". The Vienna MSS are one group, the Prague MSS another. *De penitencia* (Introd. §6): **A** Vienna
3927; **B** Bautzen, Gersdorf Library M. St. 80.7; **C** Vienna 1337; **D** Prague Univ. III G 11; **E**
Vienna 3980; **F** Vienna 4527. Cite variants with the sigla of the work you are in. Marginal notes in the
manuscripts that Loserth prints ("F in marg.", "DB in marg.") are reception: an **R** note where they
show how a reader took the text.

**The page foot** (in your brief as "apparatus", by page) has two blocks, both keyed to the **line**:
the variants (`19. C: ecclesie in marg. alia manu.`) and Loserth's source references. The OCR of it is
rough: read it for sense, not letter by letter, and read the plate foot where a variant matters.

   *secta signorum* · *novella / antiqua ecclesia* · *predicacio ydemptica / tropica* identical / tropical

   *sacramentaliter* sacramentally · *realiter* really (never "actually") · *vere* truly (never "really") ·
   *spiritualiter* spiritually · *virtualiter* virtually · *figurative* figuratively · *tropice* tropically ·
   *ydemptice* identically · *invisibiliter* invisibly · *sensibiliter* perceptibly · *corporaliter* bodily ·

   row: the compendium's *de rebus huius sacramenti* (1.3) is the three-membered distinction the chapter sets
   out, so keep its *res* consistent with that row.
2. **Connectives carry the argument.** ⛔ Never omit one, never turn one into filler.

   | Latin | English | Never |
   |---|---|---|
   | enim, nam | for | "now", "indeed" |
   | ergo, igitur, ideo | therefore, so, then | "now", "and", "indeed" |
   | sed / autem, marking the **minor premiss or next step** | now | (omitted) |
   | sed / autem, adversative | but | (omitted) |
   | autem, continuative | and | (omitted), and **not "now"** |
   | cum (concessive / causal) | although / since | "whenever" |
   | tamen | yet, nevertheless | "therefore" |
   | item | again, also | "now" |
   | vero (continuative) | and; but (adversative) | "now", "truly" |
   | verumtamen | nevertheless | "but truly" |
   | unde | hence, so | "whence" (archaic), "where" |
   | per idem | by the same token | "in the same way" |
   | ymmo | indeed, rather (correcting upward) | (omitted) |
   | patet; constat | it is clear; it is evident | |

   "Now" is reserved for the minor premiss, or the step that brings a new premiss to bear. An *autem*
   that only extends the same step is "and". ⚠ The commonest error in *De ecclesia* (close-out, ~50 of
   69 connective fixes): a *sed* that brings the minor premiss of a syllogism closed by *ergo / ideo /
   igitur* rendered "but". Before writing "but" for a *sed*, look ahead for the *ergo*: if one follows,
   it is "now".
3. **Ordinary words with a medieval sense:** GLOSSARY §D; look up any word whose classical and
   Wyclifite senses may differ, and propose additions.
4. **Syntax:** construe before rendering (main verb, subject, what every relative and genitive hangs
   on). Keep quantifier order where it is meaning. Keep Wyclif's long periods where English tolerates
   them. **A doubtful construal is never chosen silently:** translate the likelier reading and write a
   **C** note giving the alternative.
   ⚠ **Predication is the matter here, as in *De incarnacione*.** *Hoc est corpus meum*, *panis est
   corpus Christi*, *hoc sacramentum est panis*: Wyclif argues about what the pronoun demonstrates and
   in what mode the predicate holds. Keep subject and predicate in his order, keep *est* as "is" (never
   "exists", "becomes" or "represents" unless the Latin says so), and keep the mode adverb where he puts
   it (*est sacramentaliter corpus Christi* "is sacramentally the body of Christ"). ⛔ Never soften "is"
   into "signifies" or harden "figuratively" into "really": the difference is the treatise's matter.
   Demonstrative pronouns (*hoc*, *istud*) under discussion: render "this", and keep their gender
   visible in a C note where the argument turns on it (*hoc* neuter against *hic*, 348).
   **Propositions in indirect speech** whose sense is under discussion: render as the quoted proposition
   ("who say 'this is an accident without a subject'"), not "who say that …", which asserts it.

## Glossary (the 431 rows these chunks use; the full file is GLOSSARY.md)

# GLOSSARY — FROZEN 2026-09-28 (Wilson's pilot review)

*One Latin term, one English rendering, every time, across the whole corpus. A reader must be able
to recognise the same word coming back, because Wyclif's arguments turn on it. A rendering changes
only by a dated decision in CONVENTIONS' decision log, never silently in a chunk. Entries may be
*added* by recorded decision as new terms appear. **Tag keys:** a chunk tags a term as
`[English]{g:latin-form}` (CONVENTIONS §11), and the key must match a Latin form in one of the rows
below. These rows are also the source of the site's hover glossary. Where a term has two senses,
the entry says so and each chunk's note picks one. A term marked **CONTEXTUAL** holds together
senses English splits: its English is chosen by context, under the dikaiosune rule (CONVENTIONS §6).*

**Sources of the decisions.** "Pilot" = settled in `pilots/` this session. "T. p. N" = Mark
Thakkar, "Duces caecorum," *Vivarium* 58 (2020), page N (`reference/`), which documents the
errors of the two most recent translations. His corrections are adopted here unless an entry says
otherwise.

**Orthography.** Headwords are given in the Society editions' medieval spelling (*eciam*,
*racio*, *quiditas*, *ymmo*), because that is what the translator will meet on the page.

# *De eucharistia*, De eucharistia et penitencia, chapter 5: [TITLE]

## ¶1 · 338.19–339.26

> [p. 338] Videtur tangendum leviter ulterius que mala ex confessione tali per homines introducta poterint faciliter promoveri. Unum autem malum ad quod diabolus sepe seducit ecclesiam est quod homines non debite credunt in Deum Jesum Christum et sic errant fide qua crederent in Spiritum Sanctum, cum credunt remissionem peccatorum stare regulariter in iudicio prescitorum. Et sic talis confitens est maledictus qui confidit in homine, capiens sepe audaciam ad peccata talia non timendum; et sic non veretur Deum nec timet delictum in proximum et predicacio ac hortacio [p. 339] per Christum et apostolos precepte nimium sunt soporate, cum papa potest credere quod remissio peccatorum est singulariter suo iudicio reservata; et sic potest mercari cum hominibus servando sibi soli delecionem peccati a crimine. Sic optant et confessores proprii quos ipse specialiter limitat, possunt maiores et minores subvertere et servitutem diaboli introducere ac libertatem Christi paulative excludere. Et sic fratres possent mercari symoniace et observanciam mandatorum Dei tam in se quam in suis confessis perdere, ymmo possent sub sigillo confessionis ad sanctimoniales et alias mulieres accedere et sub forma confessionis seratis clausuris ipsas feminas impregnare, cum olfactus mutuus inter virum et feminam in locis absconditis ex cautela dyaboli provocat ad peccatum. Et hinc Yob pepigit fedus cum oculis suis, ut non cogitaret de virgine. Multo magis lubricus non ipsam olfaceret aut tangeret sic solitarie in loco abscondito, ymmo breviter cautela diaboli et istud medium sunt ad omnia genera criminum inductiva, ut ad peccatum diaboli, ut superbiam, invidiam atque iram, cum confessor post confessionem non audet culpam confessam prodere nec confessum suum private vel publice ad virtutem oppositam inclinare; et sic necessitatur a lege Domini Matthei XVIIIᵒ, 15 Si peccaverit in te frater tuus consenciendo crimini declinare.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶2 · 339.27–339.31

> [p. 339] Et quantum ad peccata carnalia, notum est quod istud est medium demonum ad introducendum tam gulam quam luxuriam non solum in confessores et confessos sed in personas alias per cautelas quas diabolus machinatur.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶3 · 340.1–340.28

> [p. 340] Et quantum ad peccatum mundi sive cupidinem, patet quod sacramentum istud est plenum cupidine, cum confessores tales nedum accipiunt de confessis suis pecuniam sed disponunt occulta media per que ipsam vel sibi equivalens accipient in futurum. Et una cautela novella per diabolum et Anglicos recencius hodie practisatur quod primo reservata absolucione notati criminis Romane curie mittuntur bulle quod homines possent domi absolvi, sic tamen quod fideliter calcularent quantum expenderent eundo a patria sua usque ad Romam et econtra. Et illam pecuniam darent personis mediis per quas poterit ipsis confessoribus devenire; et iustificatur per hoc istud graciosum commercium quod caritative laxatur labor peccantis quem haberet ad curiam et econtra. Et tamen hec immensa indulgencia ex plenitudine potestatis summi pontificis est promissa. Sed monoculis est satis cognitum quod ista reservacio absolucionis ad curiam est contra caritatis regulas non fundata; est insuper satis cognitum quod sequens mercacio est plena cupidine atque superbia, cum idem esset sic facere et breviter confesso dicere da mihi tantum peccuniam et securabo te quod eris apud Deum absolutus. Et revera sic in sacramento eucaristie regnat ignorancia sicut in isto; sed hic expressior infidelis cupiditas est adiuncta. Istam ergo infidelitatem fideles ecclesie Christi detegerent licet pro eius deteccione penaliter paterentur.

[ENGLISH]

# *De eucharistia*, De eucharistia et penitencia, chapter 6: [TITLE]

## ¶1 · 340.29–342.10

> [p. 340] Sed adhuc replicant quidam per exiles argucias que non forent digne memoria, nisi stulticia per diabolum fuit introducta; ideo ut ecclesia caucius caveat de illa intoxicante stulticia; videtur racionabile pro [p. 341] imprudentibus quod iste argucie per ordinem salutentur. Arguitur enim per illud evangelium dictum de decem leprosis: Ite et ostendite vos sacerdotibus. Cum ergo Christus omnia talia dicta dixerat in figura, videtur quod ex hoc dicto figuravit quod leprosi sunt specialiter a sacerdotibus confitendi. Et sic introducitur sacramentalis confessio usitata ex auctoritate evangelii. Sed pro solucione istius oportet supponere in sacramento quomodo est sensus quadruplex scripture, scilicet literalis, allegoricus, tropologicus et anagoycus. Literalis autem secundum principia Augustini habet summam auctoritatem, alii autem sensus nisi de quanto fundantur in sensu isto autentico vel evidencia racionis, quia aliter quilibet posset moralisare vel trahere auctoritatem scripture ad sensum quem vellet habere, sicut fertur quendam fratrem asseruisse quod veritas evangelica sit quecunque veritas per quam communitas salubriter est hortanda; et sic cum quelibet veritas est huiusmodi, quelibet veritas est veritas evangelica. Sic tenendo scripturam in suis limitibus, videtur quod striccius sit loquendum. Concedenda est ergo historia evangelica supradicta et dicendum quod ex ista non infertur fideliter quod homines debent in tempore gracie taliter confiteri. Sed hic videtur probabiliter concludi quod sicut in lege veteri leprosi debuerant se sacerdotibus ostendere, ut patet Luc. XVII, sic Christus autor utriusque legis approbat pro suo tempore veterem istam legem et per anterioracionem sanacionis quod ista ostensio sacerdotibus cessare debuit pro tempore legis nove. Et istud figurat reditus Samaritani qui ultra novem iudeos sanatos taliter [p. 342] est regressus. Potest eciam figurare quod spiritualiter leprosi debent currere ad sacerdotes sub lege gracie, et illi debent per verba evangelica ad salubrem cordis contricionem et peccati detestacionem inmundos spiritualiter inclinare et antea saltem naturaliter sunt ex Dei gracia expurgandi. Sicut enim non infertur ex hoc evangelio quod confitendus ferat in manu sua denarium presbitero offerendum, ita non sequitur ex ista historia quod fidelis debeat istam tradicionem hominum confiteri.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶2 · 342.11–342.18

> [p. 342] Secundo arguitur per istud Joh. XI de suscitacione Lazari, ubi postquam ligatus est institis et postquam suscitatus fuerat de sepulcro, Christus precepit suis apostolis ipsum solvere de huiusmodi ligamentis. Ex quo videtur quod Cristus docuit figuraliter quod sacerdotes sui habent potestatem solvendi spiritualiter mortuos secundum formam quam approbat usus noster.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶3 · 342.19–343.5

> [p. 342] Sed sic arguentes ab imprudenti similitudine debent primo attendere quando Jesus prius vivificavit Lazarum et fecit eum surgere de sepulcro fetido atque stare. Et tunc replicans figuram ad propositum debet concedere quod Jesus spiritualiter prius vivificat peccato sic mortuos et eos suscitans facit stare. Et postmodum fideles presbiteri debent solvere ligamenta consuetudinum peccandi sancta predicacione, quibus post delecionem peccati per Deum postea sint ligati. Sed ista consuetudinis diminucio sive solucio est presbyteris hodie pretermissa, cum nec salubribus exhortacionibus nec sanctis exemplacionibus docent populum, quando illa consuetudo peccandi est propter periculum limitanda. Et istam fidelem sentenciam debent viri apostolici noticie populi declarare et non se ipsos [p. 343] illis institis tamquam quibusdam apparenciis ypocrisis insolubiliter vinculare. Nunc autem ligant se ipsos et indisponunt ad proficiendum populo, cum obligant se diabolo propter apparenciam potestatis et lucri et non reverenter dant gloriam Deo suo.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶4 · 343.6–343.13

> [p. 343] Et per ista dicta breviter possunt solvi similes argucie per diabolum introducte. Scimus quidem quod consuetudo istorum ypocritarum novorum ordinum remurmurat contra istam sentenciam. Sed super omnia vincit veritas racionis. Non sumus autem sic desponsati cum ista sentencia quam adhuc credimus esse catholicam quin docto contrario velimus ipsam humiliter dimittere et contrarium constancius confiteri.

[ENGLISH]

# *De eucharistia*, Questio ad fratres: [TITLE]

## ¶1 · 347.3–347.19

> [p. 347] Quid in natura sua est hoc album et rotundum quod consecratur a sacerdote et quod videmus oculo corporali in manibus sacerdotis post consecracionem, quod frangitur, quod teritur dentibus, et numquid est corpus Christi, et numquid est sacramentum corporis Christi. Post publicacionem fidei scripture ex testimonio veritatis et sui Apostoli dicitur quod sacramentum eucaristie est verus panis in natura sua et cum hoc idem panis est virtute verborum sacramentalium corpus Christi, sicut est simul Deus et homo. Et solucio arguciarum in hac parte est satis facilis. Nec sunt obiectus trutannici oppositi digni memoria. Alii autem infideles taciti dicunt quod illa hostia consecrata est signum sensibile. Sed tacent infideliter utrum sit corpus Christi vel in natura sua panis, ut dicit Apostolus et sancti concorditer quousque diabolus est solutus.

[ENGLISH]

## ¶2 · 347.20–348.4

> [p. 347] Questio eiusdem: Sepe quesivit idem Johannes et adhuc queritur a fratribus utrum ista sensibilis hostia consecrata sit in natura sua panis vel unum peius quam panis equinus vel venenum, sicut fratres quidam dixerunt Oxonie, et utrum sit corpus Christi vel unums ibi accidens ignoratum, quia idem Johannes est certus ex fide quod ille panis est realiter corpus [p. 348] Domini Jesu Christi cum Christus qui mentiri non potest dicit: Hoc est corpus meum, non hic est corpus meum, cum scimus ex fide quod ubique est increata trinitas que est melior corpore Salvatoris etc.

[ENGLISH]

## For the coordinator

1. **Two of Loserth's references were missed by the notes.** P07b-5 has "Cap. VI" at 142.1, P07g-1 has "Opp. tom. VI (Ed. Migne) p. 622" at 158.9, and P07g-3 has "Cap. VI" at 159.5. Each note says there is no reference, and the changes above correct this.
2. **P07g-2.** Bede does not say Gregory set out. *Iter arripuit* follows John the Deacon (or the *Legenda aurea* tradition). Please decide whether the note should say so, as the proposed change does.
3. **P07e-8.** Gregory's text at this point reads *Laqueus ruinae populi mei*. Wyclif's *Causa sunt ruine* is the form in Gregory's letters. Please decide whether to keep this detail.
4. **P07c-2/P07f-6.** Both are the same spurious sermon (ps.-Aug. *Serm. suppos.* 72). Wyclif calls it *De verbis Domini* XIV both times.
5. **Still open.** P07b-1 (Bernard *seculariter castigandi*), P07e-4 (Chrysostom *Nudis foliis verborum*) and P07c-4 (the chroniclers) are untraced. The editor rows rest on Maurist page numbers (Chrysostom XI, Augustine V App.) that were not seen.

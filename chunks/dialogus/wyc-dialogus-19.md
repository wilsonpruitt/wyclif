# *Dialogus*, chapter 19: the friars' silence on the clergy's lordship

## Falsehood · 37.2–37.15

> [p. 37] MENDACIUM. Videtur quod innuitive accusas in isto omnes quatuor sectas cleri; sed quis auderet hoc curie romane imponere, et specialiter sectis fratrum, cum tunc regulando dominos, dominas atque regna forent proditores eorum et heretici ex consensu. Sed quis auderet sic accusare sapienciam hujus cleri? Si enim lex Christi in scriptura sacra sic diceret, verisimile est quod tot et tanti sancti de fratribus istud dominis, episcopis et abbatibus intimarent. Cum ergo in hoc tacent, ymmo impugnant istud tanquam hereticum, videtur quod nedum consenciunt, sed defendunt contrarium ut autores. Et si istud concernit salutem anime ac prosperitatem regnorum, evidens videtur quod sic similando vel asserendo forent heretici et proditores dei, hominum et regnorum.

FALSEHOOD. It seems that by implication you accuse in this all four [sects]{g:secta} of [the clergy]{g:clerus};[^1]
but who would dare to lay this on [the Roman curia]{g:curia-romana}, and especially on the sects of
the [friars]{g:frater}, since then, in governing [lords]{g:dominus}, ladies and [kingdoms]{g:regnum}, they would
be their traitors, and heretics by consent? But who would dare so to accuse the wisdom of this
clergy? For if [the law of Christ]{g:lex-christi} in holy scripture said so, it is likely that so
many and such great saints among the friars would make this known to lords, [bishops]{g:episcopus}
and abbots.[^2] Since, therefore, they are silent on this, indeed rather attack it as heretical, it
seems that they not only consent but defend the contrary, as its authors. And if this concerns the
salvation of the soul and the prosperity of kingdoms, it seems evident that by so dissembling or
asserting they would be heretics and traitors of God, of men and of kingdoms.[^3]

## Truth · 37.16–39.2

> [p. 37] VERITAS. In ista materia respondendum est a sectis et partibus quas impugnas; ego enim nescio in isto romanam curiam excusare, nec fratres aut sectas alias, sed adhuc in fide vivo domini Jesu Christi quod ipse sic vivere docuit clerum suum. Verum tamen si per impossibile fuero doctus contrarium, volo veritati catholice humiliter consentire, et omnino si doctus fuero a papa vel superioribus ecclesie ut prelatis. Sed scio quod conversacio et factum eorum non est tenendum a catholicis tanquam fides cum ipsi possunt macula avaricie infici ut mundani. Cupiditas enim infecit primum apostatam, genus humanum et alios multos lapsos. Et quantum ad quatuor sectas fratrum, ipse etatem habent, pro se ipsis respondeant, quia in hoc ipsos nescio excusare, et specialiter cum eligunt ex autoritati Christi vitam exproprietariam [p. 38] ut ceteris plus perfectam. Nec dubium quin in verbis et opere consenciunt dotacioni cleri, cum tam vecorditer in predicacionibus, in confessionibus et aliis communicacionibus tacent in isto dicere veritatem. Maledicta autem sit talis secta que ipsos necessitat ad falsum tam proditorie nutriendum. Ideo, ut quidam asserunt, fideles non communicarent cum illis, nec ave eis dicerent, antequam de isto cleri dominio palam proderint fidem suam. Si autem approbarent istud cleri dominium, tunc forent stulti sine autoritate Christi taliter mendicantes, cum foret perfeccio eis habere seculare dominium quod a Christo fuit multipliciter approbatum, et nunquam taliter mendicare, et specialiter a viris tam validis et tanto numero glomeratis, pro tantis edificiis construendis, et aliis sumptibus contra evangelium Christi fictis. Si autem ex autoritate scripture reprobant istud cleri dominium et tam multipliciter tacent ipsum hominibus quibus ex consciencia pupplicarent, quis dubitat quin sunt vir duplex animo, inconstanter tenens cum falsitate quam credit superare ad tempus modicum ut arundo? Et cum factum plus docet quam verba videtur ex professione sui ordinis quod plus est credendum suis operibus quod dominacio ista sit illicita, quam verbis suis duplicibus ad que pro temporali precio conducuntur. Nam plus est credendum medico de salubritate herbarum ab homine sicitato dicenti quod a herba est saluberrima et b herba mortifera, si a herbam aufugeret ut venenum et b herbam avide gustaverit, tanquam prandium salutare; plus, inquam, credendum est tali operi quam hiis verbis. Et sic videtur de fratribus. Sed unum scio quod fides scripture propter duplicitatem eorum non titubat. Sicut nec ordo eorum aliquam perfeccionem secte Christiane continet, nisi que ex evangelio est fundata; quod si essent usque ad illud a superfluis [p. 39] expurgate, non remaneret eis nisi clara et meritoria secta Christi.

TRUTH. In this matter answer must be made by the sects and parties that you attack; for I do not
know how to excuse the Roman curia in this, nor the friars or the other sects, but I still live in
the faith of the Lord Jesus Christ that he taught his clergy to live so. Yet if, per impossibile, I
should be taught the contrary, I am willing humbly to consent to catholic truth, and above all if I
should be taught by the [pope]{g:papa} or by the superiors of the [church]{g:ecclesia}, such as [prelates]{g:prelatus}. But I
know that their [manner of life]{g:conversacio} and deed is not to be held by catholics as faith,
since they can be infected with the stain of avarice, as worldly men are. For covetousness infected
the first apostate, the human race, and many others who have fallen. And as for the four sects of
friars, they are of age: let them answer for themselves,[^4] because in this I do not know how to
excuse them, and especially since they choose, on Christ's authority, the
[propertyless]{g:exproprietarii} life [38] as more perfect than the rest.[^5] Nor is there doubt that
in words and deed they consent to the [endowment]{g:dotacio} of the clergy, since they so
faint-heartedly keep silent from telling the truth on this in their preaching, in confessions and in
other dealings.[^6] And cursed be such a sect as compels them to nourish falsehood so traitorously.
Therefore, as some assert, the faithful should not have dealings with them, nor say "God speed" to
them, until they have openly disclosed their faith concerning this [lordship]{g:dominium} of the
clergy.[^7] Now if they approved this lordship of the clergy, then they would be fools, [to go]
[begging]{g:mendicare} in this way without Christ's authority, since it would be perfection for them
to have secular lordship, which was approved by Christ in many ways, and never to beg in this way;
and especially by men so able-bodied and massed in such numbers, for building such great edifices
and for other expenses contrived against the gospel of Christ.[^8] But if on the authority of
scripture they reject this lordship of the clergy, and in so many ways keep it silent from men to
whom in conscience they should publish it, who doubts that they are "a double minded man",
inconstantly holding with the falsehood which he believes will prevail for a little while, like a
reed?[^9] And since the deed teaches more than words, it seems from the profession of their order
that their works, [teaching] that this [exercise of lordship]{g:dominacio} is unlawful, are more to be believed than
their double words, to which they are hired for a temporal price. For a fevered man is more to
believe a doctor, about the wholesomeness of herbs, who tells him that herb a is most wholesome and
herb b deadly, if he fled herb a as poison and eagerly tasted herb b as a wholesome meal: more, I say,
is such a deed to be believed than these words.[^10] And so it seems with the friars. But one thing I
know: that the faith of scripture does not totter because of their doubleness. Just as their order
contains no perfection of the Christian sect except what is founded on the gospel; and if they were
[39] purged of superfluities down to that, nothing would remain to them but the clear and
meritorious sect of Christ.[^11]

## Pollard's side-notes (PD, verbatim; not our translation)

- 37.2: Mend. objects:
- 37.5: If endowments are bad and friars hide this from their rich penitents, this makes them
  traitors and heretics!
- 37.16: Ver. answers:
- 37.17: They must make their own defence.
- 37.28: Friars cannot be excused for choosing the unendowed life and yet approving endowments.
- 38.9: If the secular power of the clergy is right, why are friars beggars?
- 38.15: If wrong, their silence is hypocritical.
- 38.21: But their life requires that it should be wrong, and this is most to be trusted.
- 38.29: Purged of its unscriptural attributes their sect would be acceptable to God.

## Notes

[^1]: **R.** *Quatuor sectas cleri*: Wyclif's later writings reckon four sects in the clergy (the
    endowed secular clergy, monks, canons and friars); *quatuor sectas fratrum* (37.27) are the four
    mendicant orders, Franciscans, Dominicans, Carmelites and Austin friars (LEDGER D19-1, D19-2).

[^2]: **C.** *Dominis, episcopis et abbatibus*: taken with Pollard's commas as a series. Without them
    *dominis episcopis* would be "the lord bishops" (GLOSSARY §B), leaving "to the lord bishops and
    abbots".

[^3]: **T.** *Similando*: taken as *simulando*, "dissembling", the friars' silence of the preceding
    sentence. B reads *asimulando*, B¹, C *asimilando*.

[^4]: **S/C.** John 9:21, *etatem habet, ipse de se loquatur* ("he is of age: let him speak for
    himself"), turned into the plural and to *respondeant*. *Ipse* is plural here (= *ipsi*).

[^5]: **T.** *Ex autoritati Christi*: printed so, the ablative in *-i*; B, B¹, C read *sibi* for
    *Christi*.

[^6]: **C.** *Vecorditer*: "faint-heartedly", the medieval sense of *vecors*; the classical sense,
    "senselessly", would also serve.

[^7]: **S/T.** *Nec ave eis dicerent*: 2 John 10, *nec Ave ei dixeritis*, in DR's "God speed".
    *Proderint* is printed; C reads *prodiderint*, "have disclosed", which is translated.

[^8]: **C/T.** *A viris tam validis et tanto numero glomeratis*: taken as the agents of the begging,
    the friars themselves (able-bodied, massed in convents). *A* could instead mean "from": begging
    from men so powerful. B, C read *in aliis sumptibus* for *et aliis sumptibus*.

[^9]: **S/T.** James 1:8, *vir duplex animo inconstans est in omnibus viis suis*; Wyclif keeps the
    singular after *sunt*. *Ut arundo* recalls the "reed shaken with the wind" (Matt. 11:7). B, B¹, C
    read *constanter*, "constantly holding with the falsehood", for *inconstanter*.

[^10]: **T/C.** *Sicitato*, printed, gives no word; B, B¹, C read *febricitato*, "fevered", which is
    translated, with *ab homine* as the agent of *credendum*. B, B¹, C read *gustaret* for
    *gustaverit*.

[^11]: **C.** *Expurgate* is feminine plural: its subject is the friars' sects (*secte*), understood
    from *ordo eorum*. *Usque ad illud*: down to what is founded on the gospel.
